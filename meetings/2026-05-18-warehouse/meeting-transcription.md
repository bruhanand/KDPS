### 3. Meeting Summary

The conversation revolves around digitizing the manual invoicing and inventory data entry processes at KDPS, specifically focusing on the creation of Product Tracker (PT) files from various vendor invoices.

- **The Ingestion Challenge:** The business receives invoices in 10-15 different formats from 40+ brands and local vendors. Currently, this relies heavily on manual data entry and mapping.

- **Branded vs. Non-Branded Workflows:**
- **Branded Goods:** Companies like Aditya Birla provide structured PT files every 15 days, which standardizes data extraction slightly, though formats still vary.

- **Non-Branded Goods:** For local/non-branded items (e.g., sarees, kids' wear, kurtis), vendors often provide basic invoices lacking barcodes, sizes, or Maximum Retail Prices (MRPs).

- **Manual Workarounds for Non-Brands:** To process untagged items, staff currently call the vendor directly to confirm the item type and size. They then manually calculate the MRP by adding a 1.1% transportation cost, GST, and a 30-35% profit margin (while branded goods have a margin closer to 25%).

- **AI Implementation Plan:** The AI agent needs to be educated over several months to understand and extract data from these varied formats. Because some details exist only in phone conversations and not on the invoice, the developers discuss the hurdle of training the AI to generate accurate PT files, custom barcodes, and pricing parameters automatically.

- **Future Scope:** While the initial phase will require heavy manual verification to train the system, future phases (planned for after 3 months) will include advanced AI-driven features like demand forecasting and predictive stock ordering based on historical sales cycles.

### **Business Process Summary**

Based on the meeting transcript, here is a structured breakdown of the core operational challenges and the need for system automation:

**1. The Ingestion & PT File Bottleneck**

- **Manual Mapping & Creation:** Currently, tracking goods upon arrival is a highly manual process. For non-branded items, staff (like Mahendra ji) must manually count, interpret, write details, and create PT (Product Tracker) files from scratch. Even for branded items, warehouse staff spends hours calling, messaging, and emailing vendors to procure PT files.
- **Data Errors:** The legacy Excel sheets are filled with data hygiene issues (e.g., spelling variations like "pajama" vs. "plazo", inconsistent spacing, and mixed variables in the 'Gender' or 'Color' columns). A single spelling mistake disrupts reporting across the board.

**2. Store Distribution & POS Friction**

- **Bifurcation:** Goods are inwarded at the warehouse and then manually allocated to stores based on perceived historical performance (e.g., sending 60% of stock to a high-performing store). The team wants the new system to use AI to automatically suggest these allocations based on data.
- **System Lag & Login Issues:** Store managers scan barcodes to officially receive goods via their interface. However, the 5-7-year-old POS system is painfully slow. To check reports, the operations lead has to individually log in and out of 5+ different store accounts just to compile data, rather than having a global dashboard with a dropdown menu.
- **Barcode Errors:** Items (like Jockey) frequently throw "Stock not available" errors due to multiple MRPs attached to the same barcode. This forces the staff to perform "manual billing" to satisfy the waiting customer while the backend team scrambles to fix the system entry.

**3. Promotional Offers & Point of Sale Fraud**

- **Lack of Centralized Offers:** Currently, the system cannot natively enforce brand-specific End of Season Sales (EOSS) or tiered discounts (e.g., 40% off Van Heusen jackets). Instead, placards are placed in the store, and salesmen must apply the discounts manually.
- **Theft Loophole:** Because discounts are manual, a salesman can offer a customer a 20% discount on an item that is eligible for a 40% discount, pocket the 20% difference, and manipulate the billing system (or use MRP-to-MRP exchanges to cover their tracks).
- **The Fix:** Hardcoding discounts to the barcode level in the new system is an absolute priority to "choke" this loophole and remove the salesman's ability to manipulate the final price.

**4. The Reporting "Crunch Week"**

- **Grueling Deadlines:** From the 1st to the 7th of every month, the core management team works 18-19 hour days to manually cross-check every single sale against brand promotional offers to generate commission and sales reports.
- **Financial Dependency:** If these reports are not submitted to the brands on time, the brands will delay KDPS's commission payouts, which subsequently halts employee payroll. This makes the manual reporting an exhausting but unavoidable necessity.
- **Automation Goal:** The team acknowledges that doing this manually when "we have so much technology at our disposal" is a waste of human capital. The upcoming AI tools are desperately needed to automate daily WhatsApp sales digests, attendance, and commission reports.

### Part III: Business Process Summary & Action Plan

Based on the meeting, here is a structured summary of the operational bottlenecks and the planned AI system solutions:

**1. The WhatsApp & PT File Bottleneck**

- **The Problem:** Currently, stores constantly request PT files via WhatsApp as new stock arrives. This requires a fragile 4-person chain (Generation $\rightarrow$ Verification $\rightarrow$ Inwarding $\rightarrow$ Billing) to process. If one person is on leave, operations freeze.
- **Data Friction:** Relying on WhatsApp Web leads to lost files ("could not find message") and massive time wasted scrolling to match barcodes.

**2. Image Quality & Ingestion Workflow**

- **The Problem:** Store staff frequently upload blurry or crooked photos of paper invoices, forcing the head office to manually fix them in Photoshop before they can be processed.
- **The Solution:** Implement strict front-end validation on the store's handheld device. If an image is unreadable, the system will instantly reject it ("Data is not clear, redo it") and prevent upload. The app will automatically convert valid photos to PDFs and rename them systematically (e.g., Invoice Name + Brand) to eliminate the current messy folder structures.

**3. Vendor & Agent CRM**

- **The Problem:** Tracking shipments, defective items, and returns is difficult. For example, if 100 items are billed but only 96 usable ones arrive, manual intervention is needed.
- **The Solution:** Build a dedicated CRM for Vendor and Agent management. Booking agents can log into the system to verify physical stock against system entries, instantly log defects, and initiate ledger updates or returns seamlessly.

**4. Consumer Psychology & Store Layout Strategy**

- **Insight:** KDPS actively structures store layouts based on shopping behavior. At the Sanskar multi-brand outlet, women's apparel drives 70-72% of total sales.
- **Strategy:** Because men view clothing as a necessity and women shop frequently for various occasions (up to 12 times a year), the ground floor is entirely allocated to female apparel to capture immediate convenience and impulse buying, while men's sections are placed on the upper floors.
