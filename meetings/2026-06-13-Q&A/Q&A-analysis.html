Q&A Call — Requirements Clarification

A long question-and-answer call where Anand asked the KDPS team to clear up how the business actually runs, before module design continues. This page captures every question, the answer given, and what it means for the system.
Date: 13 June 2026 Type: Q&A / requirements ~40 questions covered Next meet: tomorrow, ~12–1 PM (with Priyo)

The 6 biggest things that came out of this call

  • SOR = Consignment. Same thing, two names. Unsold stock goes back, no cap on return %.
  • A third stock model exists: "Correction" (collection). The brand gives a % allowance calculated on delivered quantity, not booked quantity. So "buy & sell" has 4 flavours: with-correction, without-correction, SOR, consignment.
  • Tag the supplier, not the brand. One supplier can carry many brands (e.g. Madura = Allen Solly, Louis Philippe, Van Heusen, AS Kids).
  • Models change over time — design flexible, no hard constraints. A separate "what can change vs what is fixed" discussion is planned for next week.
  • Ten Software has an API and will share it + docs. Barcodes are generated inside Ten. Tally stays the accounting book.
  • Everything front-of-store (exchange, defective, split payment, manual discount) is already handled by the POS; the system pulls it via API/report — it doesn't re-do it.

1Stock ownership models

The single most important clarification of the call. There are three models, and "buy & sell" turned out to have four flavours.
QIn SOR, what is the return %? Is "10%" fixed?
AThe brand says 10%, but KDPS stretches it — up to 12% on some brands, up to 15% on others. It is a percentage of the stock sent that season (e.g. 1,000 pieces sent over Spring-Summer → 10% of 1,000).
QIs SOR the same as Consignment?
AYes — same concept, parallel terms. SOR = "Sale or Return". Goods belong to the brand; whatever doesn't sell is returned, with no cap (10/20/30% — it doesn't matter). Old/new/season is not checked — leftover pieces go back as-is. The minor difference described: SOR = one lot, return the unsold; Consignment = repeated top-ups, return whatever is unsold across lots. For design purposes, treat them as one model.
QSo what is the "Correction" / collection model?
AA second model. The brand gives a % allowance (10/12/20%, varies) — but it is calculated on the quantity actually serviced (delivered), not booked. Booked 1,000, delivered 900 → correction is on 900. Booked 800, sold them, took 200 more → correction is on 1,000 (the total delivered). Levi's does a variant based on actual buying.
Design implication: Stock-ownership model is a first-class dimension (already a domain rule). Capture four flavours: SOR/Consignment, Correction (on delivered qty), without-correction, plus brand-specific variants like Levi's actual-buying. The return-% cap is a per-brand stretchable number (10 → 12 → 15), not a constant. SOR return is calculated on season stock sent; correction is calculated on delivered quantity — two different bases, both must be derivable.

2Invoices & PT file

QThe invoice comes with the goods and has style code, size, quantity, price — right?
ANot consistently. Different invoices carry different fields. Some have no style code at all — just "this product, this brand, this many pieces". Colour may or may not be there; size may or may not be there.
QThen how is a PT file made when the invoice has no style code? Is a new code generated?
AThe style code usually exists elsewhere — on the challan, or written on the box. It gets added into the PT file at the moment the PT file is created, so it isn't a blocking problem.
Design implication: The PT-file maker can't assume invoice fields. Source of style/size/colour can be invoice or challan or box label. The PT file is the normalisation point where missing identifiers are filled in. (Reinforces the existing pdf-to-pt blueprint.)

3Brand incentives, margins & offers

QDo brands give incentives to KDPS?
AYes, definitely — but highly dynamic, many parameters, different per brand. Example: "open 6 of my stores → 2% extra commission; open 10 stores → 3% extra." Can be documented per brand on request.
Where it's used: Anand placed this in the future cash audit flow — brand incentive feeds the cash/settlement reconciliation later, not the inbound flow.
QDiscounts are per-brand and change often?
AYes, dynamic — some brands change every week. KDPS can supply a month's snapshot (e.g. "May offers, all brands"). Anand asked for the May brand-offers file.

4Supplier vs Brand — which to tag?

QThere's a supplier and a brand. Which do we tag stock against?
ATag the supplier. One supplier can deliver multiple brands, so the supplier is the anchor. "Don't say brand, say supplier." KDPS will provide a supplier-with-brand mapping and a sample supplier PT file.
Design implication: Supplier is the parent entity in the booking/inbound model; brand is an attribute that can be many-per-supplier. This affects how PT files, margins, return windows and payment terms are keyed.

5Booking & recording non-brand orders

QDo suppliers/vendors cancel bookings?
AGenerally not for brands. It happens with non-brands. They may send a different quantity (booked 100, sent 90 + 10 of something else) but a full zero-cancellation hasn't been seen.
QHow do we record non-brand bookings (which currently go unrecorded)?
AFor non-brand, the invoice that arrives becomes the booking record ("what came is what I booked"). Anand proposed capturing the order at order-time via the app with three input options: text, voice recording, or chat — because non-brand orders happen over WhatsApp call/chat with no document. The team agreed to try it but could not guarantee it → flagged as next-phase / planning only.

6Inbound split & store transfer

QWhen stock is split store-wise, is the PT file made per-store or once?
APT file is created first (once) → uploaded to the software → then distribution/sorting decides which store gets what (done by whoever is responsible at the time, even over the phone). Four destinations → four separate stocks. That stock reaches Mahendra Ji, who bills it under each store's name and dispatches via transport.
Deliverable requested: the warehouse-to-store transfer bill format (the document Mahendra Ji generates when billing to a store).

7Transport cost & a direct-shipping idea

QWho bears the transport cost from vendor/brand to KDPS?
A~90% borne by KDPS; a few vendors bear it themselves. Per-vendor / per-consignment transport-cost tracking is wanted and considered doable (ties to the booking register).
Q(Idea raised mid-call) Can stock skip the warehouse and ship store-to-destination directly?
AYes — to avoid double courier cost (e.g. Banka → HQ → Surat) and time lag, the ERP can let the source store do GR directly and ship to the destination, tracked centrally via consignment/docket number + image, with a debit note issued from HQ. Reduces warehouse GR load. Noted as a real future capability — reason it must stay flexible (direction can change from either side).

8Barcode & Ten Software API

QWhat tool generates barcodes?
ATen Software generates barcodes. KDPS has already spoken with Ten (in Dhanbad); Ten offered to build software but KDPS declined (half done already). Ten confirmed they have an API and will share it — integration expected to be easy. KDPS told Ten their own ERP is accounting-based, which Ten lacks, so the two are complementary.
Deliverable requested: Ten Software API + API documentation. (Matches the standing assumption that the client supplies the POS API.)

9Selling before scan / POS down

QIf an item is sold before it's scanned-in (direct brand→store or warehouse→store), how is it handled?
AManual billing — barcode written by hand, added later. When time permits and the item is inwarded, the proper bill is generated.
QInter-store movement — is "SOH" sent?
A"SOH" is the wrong term. A system-generated, PT-file-like transfer document is sent (POS generates it), and its data is extracted to Excel. If the receiving store doesn't scan-in the barcodes, the SOH goes negative there.
QIf POS is down, a handwritten bill = ?
ASame as a manual bill.
Deliverable requested: sale export sample (POS) and SOH format — Anand wants these to structure the data before the API docs arrive.

10Payments — split, UPI, credit

QDo part-payments happen (some cash + some UPI)?
AHandled manually. UPI is taken on the store manager's device, not the company system — the company only cares that payment was made, not by whom. Splits are allowed (e.g. ₹1,000 item, ₹500 now / ₹500 next visit; manager fronts it and deposits to the company QR).
QIs credit / udhaar allowed?
AAbsolutely not. No credit at all.

11Discounts

QManual discount — is it handled later in the report?
AYes. The sale export shows the discount as a % (or as an amount if no %). It's all in the exported data, handled at report time.
QHow do non-brand discounts work?
AMostly "Buy One Get One" in-shop, otherwise no fixed structure — case-by-case (e.g. 5–10% to lock in a loyal/potential customer, within margin). Stores may discount up to ~5% at their discretion; beyond that needs a call + approval. No report is needed for non-brand (it's KDPS's own stock — just audit it; no claim to make from a brand). Floor rule: don't sell a ₹400 item for ₹200.
QAnd brand discounts?
AFixed by the brand (which items, how much) — brand sends it; KDPS inputs the offers from the back-end. Front-end handles ad-hoc; report reconciles the rest.
Design implication: Discount handling is layered — front-end (POS) for ad-hoc + non-brand, back-end offer rulebook for brand offers, report-time reconciliation for claims. Non-brand is profit-only (audit, no claim); brand discounts feed claims back to the brand. This is the D5 offer-rulebook + report territory.

12Exchange & defective items

QWhat is the store exchange window?
A5 days printed on the receipt, but stretched to 10–12 days for repeat/loyal customers. → Treat as soft, not a hard block.
QDefective item flow (per model)?
A
  • Manufacturing defect → no return-window hassle. Inspected, accepted, exchanged for equal-or-higher value.
  • Used item → must be within the return window (5–7 days).
  • No tag → still accepted; details pulled from bill / barcode.
  • Bill lost → look up the customer by phone number (name can repeat, number generally doesn't) to retrieve date, items, value.
All of this is POS front-end work.
QWhere do defective pieces go back?
AAll defectives route through the warehouse — branded or non-branded, whatever path they came in by. The warehouse then runs the goods-return process to the brand (defective / correction / SOR / consignment).
QSpecial case — Madura pickup?
AMadura (Allen Solly, Louis Philippe, Van Heusen, Allen Solly Kids) picks up directly from the store — their vehicle, their cost, KDPS pays nothing. Only Madura does this. Treat as a general "supplier-picks-from-store" capability that today only one supplier uses (and could change).

13Inter-store shortage & transit damage

QStore A sends 100, Store B physically gets 95 — what happens?
AReceiver (Hazaribagh) accepts all 100 on the system, and sends the 5 short pieces back on paper to the sending store (Ranchi), which then investigates the short shipment.
QAnd transit damage?
ANon-branded → KDPS absorbs the loss. Branded → brand owners are emailed to recover as much as possible.

14Counting

QWhen does counting happen?
AAt stock receipt, and additionally a "Global Counting" every Monday — a stock count of a particular brand, at store level (not warehouse). Done more for branded, but also non-branded.

15Cash, accounts & expenses

QHow is cash deposited?
AA CIS agency picks up the cash and deposits it. KDPS has 2 bank accounts. Detailed cash-flow / account-mapping questions go to the Patna accounts manager (madam) on a separate con-call — Anand to prepare an accounting questionnaire (card settlements, totals, which stores → which account, etc.).
QStore-expense audit?
ANo formal audit. An online "Petty Cash" Excel sheet per store; staff log expenses, request top-ups when low, and KDPS verifies entries are genuine (no out-of-the-way items). Can be handled within documentation.

16Users, stores & access

QHow many users / what access levels?
ARoughly one user per store, plus role-based access (KD Uncle, Priyo, Anand all have different access). Anand to draft a user-list + privileges map. KDPS to send the store list (how many stores currently added).

17Dead stock, Tally, incentives, old data

QHow is old / dead stock handled — what defines "dead"?
ANeeds an aging method; how to classify dead is pending — to be decided with Priyo Sir at the next meeting.
QTally import?
ATo be asked of the Patna office. (Tally remains the statutory book.)
QWho gets sales incentives?
AOnly store-level sales staff and store managers. Management has none. Incentive logic must be includable in the system.
QOld data — is the PT file the source of truth?
ANo — the PT file is NOT the source of truth. When KDPS shares the SOH format, filtering it will reveal where the discrepancies are; they'll highlight the problem columns. Important input for the migration (D9) discussion.
