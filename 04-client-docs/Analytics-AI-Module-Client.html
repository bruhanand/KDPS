Analytics & Intelligence Module

The "brain" of the system — it watches every store every day, reports on WhatsApp in plain language, and helps you decide. It never moves money or stock on its own; a person always decides.

Please confirm. Today KDPS has no analytics — every report is built by hand. Below is exactly what this module will give you and how. Read each point and tell us anything that does not match how you actually work, so we can correct it before building.

1.The big idea

  • One trusted set of numbers. Sales, stock, margin, ageing — each is worked out once, the same way everywhere, so every report agrees instead of contradicting.
  • It only reads and reports. It sends messages, reports and suggestions. It never changes a price, pays a bill, or edits the accounts — a person always approves.
  • It meets you on WhatsApp. Owners don't have to log into anything; the key numbers and warnings come to your phone.
  • Honest, then smart. It starts with simple, dependable rules and gets cleverer as it sees more of your own sales — no empty "AI" promises.

2.First, it cleans up the data

  • Today a single brand is recorded under many codes (Louis Philippe shows as LP, LR, LY, LA…) and many items have the wrong season on them. Any report built on that is unreliable.
  • The system maps everything to one clean structure: Supplier → Brand → Sub-brand (e.g. Aditya Birla → Van Heusen → V Dot), so brand totals are finally correct, and you can still drill into a sub-brand.
  • It shows a "data trust" figure — e.g. "94% of Louis Philippe items matched cleanly" — so you always know how solid a number is, and watch it improve.

3.The daily WhatsApp report

  • One short message at 9 PM — the day's close. Built to be read in seconds, not a wall of numbers.
  • A small summary on top (in percentages — vs target, vs last week), the money lower down, a store leaderboard, one "unusual today" line, and a short "needs a look" list you can tap into.

4.The right alert to the right person

  • Each person gets only what is theirs — finance is not bothered with shop-floor issues, and the floor is not shown cash matters.
  • If something important is not acted on, it climbs up to the next person, and finally to the owner.

5.The monthly brand report — made automatically

  • The Sale (vouchers) and SOH (stock) sheets you send today, per brand per store, in the exact same format — but generated in one click instead of by hand.
  • No more gathering each shop's file, fixing bill numbers, or applying discounts by hand — the system already holds clean sales, real bill numbers and the discount that was given.
  • Month-end target management: where a brand's report should line up to its monthly target, the system helps balance it across two neighbouring months and you approve it. (Your statutory books in Tally stay exactly as they are.)

6.Profit by brand and by store

  • Worked out automatically — cost from the PT at stock-in, sale price from the POS — never typed by hand.
  • Owned stock and SOR / consignment stock are judged differently (on SOR you risk no money, so the question is "is this brand worth the shelf space?", not "return on stock").
  • A simple scorecard shows which brands and stores to lean into vs pull back, and a "where did the margin go" view splits any discount into mark-down, free gifts and returns.
  • Full store profit after rent and salaries (pulled from Tally) can be added later.

7.Dead stock & mark-down help

  • It finds stock that is old and not selling, and "broken sizes" — where a style's popular sizes are sold out and only odd sizes are left (a hidden loss, because customers leave without buying).
  • For each, it suggests the right move: return to the brand (if within the window), move to a store where it sells, or mark it down — with a safe price floor so you never sell below an agreed margin.
  • It only suggests; the Account Manager and store decide.

8.The money watchdog

  • Catches discount given beyond the offer rules (the biggest gap today, where discounts are keyed by hand), cash deposited short, and data mistakes (cost above MRP, stuck transfers).
  • Ranks the worst stores — and even a per-cashier figure — so a problem is easy to spot.
  • It flags for a person to check; it never blocks a sale or accuses anyone. A flag means "look here", and the person confirms or clears it.

9.Selling & stock forecasting

  • What's selling fast, what to re-order, and which stock to move between stores — automating the judgement you make today by feel.
  • For brand-new styles it learns from similar past styles; it builds size patterns per region so you buy the right size mix.
  • Honest note: for new products this is a guide to "narrow the decision", not an exact number — it sharpens as a couple of clean seasons build up. The fully automatic "demand AI" is a later stage, once there is enough clean history.

10.It explains itself in plain Hindi

  • The numbers are worked out by the system; the plain-language line on top ("sales up 12%, Patna led, Ranchi slow on a stock-out") is written in Hindi or English.
  • Later, a "just ask" assistant can be added on top so anyone can ask a question in their own words and get the answer for their stores — built to plug in without re-doing the system.
