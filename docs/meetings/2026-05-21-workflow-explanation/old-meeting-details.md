The discussion covers evaluating a flowchart/workflow for converting vendor physical or digital invoices into standardized **Product Tracker (PT)** files to update internal retail systems at KDPS. The primary issue is automating data entry across vastly contrasting workflows (Branded vs. Non-Branded).

## 4. Identified Review Comments & System Constraints

During the run-through of the operational flows, the following system limitations and process adjustments were noted:

* 
**Absence of SKUs on Initial Invoices:** Invoices function purely as logistics confirmation that a delivery occurred. They indicate items passed down to multi-brand units under Store-in-Store (SIS) frameworks but do not contain active SKUs.


* 
**The Phone-Call Information Trap:** For local vendors, key missing data strings (such as size configurations or item types like Patiala vs. Kurti set) are resolved strictly over unrecorded phone conversations. An offline AI model scanning an invoice document cannot automatically discover data that only exists verbally.


* 
**Dynamic Barcoding Variables:** While single barcodes usually map cleanly, exceptions exist where identical historic barcodes carry updated price variances. The architecture must prevent quantities from breaking or jumbling up rows during system input.



---

## 5. Next Action Plan & AI Architecture Goals

1. **Iterative AI Training Path:** Implement a **2-Phase Approach**. During the initial **3 months**, focus entirely on training data extraction templates for the varying vendor invoice layouts. Operators will continue handling pricing logic and exceptions manually to "educate" the model.


2. 
**Phase 2 Advanced Modules:** Predictive analytics, sales cycle alignment, historical trend evaluations (e.g., assessing past cycle demand to reorder stock), and automated ERP generation will be introduced after month 3, once clean data sets are established.


3. 
**Evaluating Communication Auditing:** To solve the non-branded data gap, the team recommended exploring an option to **monitor or transcribe warehouse coordination calls** via AI. This will allow the system to cross-reference conversation transcripts with raw Tally files and automatically generate output rows.
