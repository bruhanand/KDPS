## PART A · Store Workflow Discussion

The owner walked through a day-in-the-life of one store. The flow he stepped through, in order:

> **Inward → SOH check → Billing → Outbound transfer → Seasonal swap / returns / brand re-order → Cross-cutting access & architecture**

### A1. Inward — stock arriving at the store

**Sources of stock**
- Daily inward is the norm.
- Stock comes from (a) the **central warehouse in Ranchi**, (b) **other stores** (e.g. Bhagalpur), (c) rarely cross-shipment store-to-store.
- All non-brand barcoding / tagging / sorting happens at the **Ranchi warehouse**. PT files themselves are **generated by people** (manual work); they are **entered into the current system at the Patna office**. Branded items can ship warehouse→store or store→store.
- Demo case: 55-piece inbound from Bhagalpur, pre-generated for the demo.

**The "in" process at the store**
1. Open the inward voucher (already visible as pending the moment the source store/warehouse created the outbound PT).
2. Staff scans **each piece's barcode** — every individual item carries its own barcode.
3. With each scan, **Received qty** ↑, **Pending qty** ↓.
4. When all pieces are scanned, the inward is complete and SOH auto-adjusts.

**Pain point — selling before "in"**
- Physically, the goods are in the staff's hand → they can sell before keying the inward in.
- Consequence: that SKU shows **negative balance** in the SOH report until the inward is keyed in. On the next sync it plus-minuses to the correct figure.
- Owner's words (translated): *"The store has handed it to me, I can bill it — but because I haven't done 'in' in my system, SOH will show it negative."*

---

### A2. SOH (Stock on Hand) report

**Where it lives**
- Path in the software: **Reports → Retail Analysis → Stock Query**.
- Scoped **per login** — i.e. the store you logged in as.
- Filterable by **all items** or **a single brand**.
- Output: **Excel**.

**How SOH is built**
- Constructed from the **PT file** issued by the Patna office to that store.
- Updates are near-real-time in the cloud — every sale/scan reflects immediately.

**Columns shown**
- Opening Quantity · Purchase · Sale · Adjustments · Transfers · Receipt · Total Quantity
- Per-row metadata: MRP, Rate, Season of in-take, Barcode, Supplier.

**Example given by the owner**
- "Gave 5 → none sold → currently 5."
- "Gave 5 → sold 1 → currently 4."
- "Gave 5 → transferred 2 → sold 2 → plus some came from somewhere else → all subtotals roll up at the bottom."
- The report spans **store-opening date → today** — SKUs that ran out are still listed at zero.

**Pain points**
| # | Pain | Owner's fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Too much info for store staff** — history, supplier, season, MRP, prior transfers. They only care about what's physically in hand. | Strip the store-level SOH down to: stock came → I received → if I sold without receiving show negative → once received, auto-adjust. |
| 2 | **Bandwidth waste** — every fetch pulls a huge dataset from the master DB. | Per-store data scope; only this store's records. |
| 3 | **No role-based access** — every login (store user, sub-master, master) sees the same data. Quote: *"Sabke paas full access hai, sabke paas saara information hai."* | Role-scoped data + role-scoped UI. |

---

### A3. Billing / sale (high-level here, details in Part B)

- Same barcode that was inwarded is scanned at the POS.
- Discount is a **manual %** typed each time.
- Owner's explicit wish: at EOSS time, **the system should auto-populate the max allowed discount per SKU/barcode** (e.g. "this SKU = 20% only"). Counter staff should have zero discretion and zero mental overhead. His words: *"Iska sardardi hi khatam — dekhna nahi padega ki kitna discount, direct milega."*
- Rule is set by HO; counter staff just *chooses*.

---

### A4. Stock Transfer OUT — to other stores / warehouse

**Triggers (any one is enough; "purpose does not need to be defined")**
1. **Sister store needs stock** — their size/style is out while a customer is waiting; stock moves; bill happens at the *receiving* store (e.g. customer in Hazaribagh, transfer from Patna, bill in Hazaribagh).
2. **Slow-movement rebalancing** — an SKU has been sitting 3–4 months at one store but sells well at another.
3. **Seasonal swap** — winter out → summer in (and vice versa) because floor space is limited. *"Space limited hai, oopar neeche nahi ho sakta."*
4. **Floor-space relief** — clearing room for new arrivals.

**MBO vs EBO distinction (important)**
- Internal stock transfer is **only for MBOs and warehouse-connected stores**.
- **EBOs do NOT do internal transfer** — that's between the brand company and the EBO. *"Hum log us lafde mein nahi padte hain."*

**How a transfer is created**
- Panel: **Stock Transfer**.
- Select destination store from a dropdown of the owner's stores.
- Scan each item by barcode. For some brands (e.g. Jockey) entry is by **style code** because that's how the brand's PT file is structured (owner said he'll explain the why later).
- On **Save Voucher**, a PT file is generated and instantly appears as a *pending inward* on the destination store's login. Receiving store scans it in when goods physically arrive.

**Pain points (owner demoed live — calls the current software "almost broken" for this flow)**
| # | Pain point | Fix / request |
|---|---|---|
| a | **Every barcode scan = synchronous online lookup against the master DB.** Multi-second latency per scan. First scan slowest. | **Offline / local lookup against this store's records only.** *"Yeh to fetch karna to hone hi nahi tha, yeh to offline hona tha pura."* |
| b | **Rapid repeat scan throws Unhandled Exception popup** — race condition. | Fix the race; don't crash on rapid scans. |
| c | **After qty entry, cursor drops to the next row** instead of returning to the barcode input. | Auto-return cursor to the barcode field after qty + Enter. *"Maine koi complex coding ki, kari hi nahi…agar entry ho gaya, tum wapas yahan barcode pe lekar chale jao."* |
| d | Because cursor lands wrong, **the next scanned barcode lands in the qty cell** → "Stock not enough". | Either fix (c) OR make the barcode field the only field that accepts barcode input. |
| e | **Long lists scroll the active SKU out of view.** During a 400–500 piece migration there's no visible per-SKU running tally. | Keep the active row visible; show **running tally per SKU**. |
| f | If a mistake happens mid-way through a 400-piece transfer, the only recovery is to re-count physically or delete and start over. *"200 ke baad agar gadbadi ho gaya to mera halat kya hoga socho to."* | Per-line correction; persistent visible tally; undo. |
| g | **No loading indicator** while data fetches → staff click again thinking nothing happened → double entries. | Visible buffering / loading state. |
| h | "Enter city" popup appears where the user expects to type quantity. | Field labels / popup flow needs cleanup. |

**Concrete current case**
- The **Ratu store is closing.** Owner is moving 400–450 pieces out via this broken flow right now.

### A7. Re-ordering / booking (brand demand workflow)

- Booking for next season happens months ahead — **SS26 winter booking done in Feb 26**.
- Owner's re-order heuristic: *"Ek aadmi ko ek suit pasand karwane ke liye kam se kam uske paas 25–30 hona chahiye."* When designs available in a category drop below ~30, he places a fresh order.
- Order placed via **direct call / email** to the brand. Reps stay in touch.
- Brand reps invite him to the **Ranchi warehouse** to see new models; he books there. Goods arrive **1.5–2 weeks** later via factory → company → store.

**Request → CRM module**
- *"Yeh CRM aayega, customer relation wala…wahin pe type kar diye, ab khud se mail kar diya."*
- Auto-detect "stock low for this brand at this store" → auto-draft / auto-send a re-order email to the brand contact.

### A8. Demand prediction — what's predictable and what isn't (his frame)

**Predictable**
- **Design, colour, trend.**
- **Season.**

**Not predictable**
- **Style** — trends shift suddenly. He cited the *Dhurandhar* movie hype — stock came in, didn't pick up (especially in Muslim areas).
- **Footfall.** *"Customer aayega nahi aayega…baraf girega, ola girega, baarish girega, Covid ho jayega — kuch nahi kar sakte."*

**Geographic / area sensitivity**
- Same brand, same product behaves differently across stores.
- Example: **Kurti** — Patna average bills ₹2,000–2,500; same kurti in **Banka** bills ₹1,000–1,500.
- Pricing and demand are **area + crowd + price-point sensitive**, not just store-sensitive.

**Stated pricing posture**
- "We are a **no-discount store**" — target is 100% MRP.
- Customers are adamant → discounts get negotiated.
- He **explicitly avoids %** in front of customers and prefers cash-amount framing — customers perceive "₹500 off" as bigger than "10% off" even when the math is identical. *"Saiki ke sath khelna padta hai."*

---

### A9. Customer wallet / loyalty points

- POS already accrues points on every purchase.
- **Redemption has never worked** — wallet screen throws *"Invalid message text"* and asks for an OTP that never arrives. Owner confirms this is a software bug, never fixed by the vendor.
- Some customers are sitting on **20,000–30,000 unredeemed points**.
- For years the store has been telling customers "regarding points, talks are going on."

**Request for the new system**
- Real redemption with a clear rule (e.g. **5 points = ₹1**, or **1,000 points = ₹500 off**).
- Cited his prior experience at **Kidder England**: "₹9,000 bill → redeem ₹1,500 → customer more than happy."
- Feel-good factor for regulars. Doesn't have to be large amounts.
- Should be redeemable at end-of-bill, optionally.

---

### A10. Printer / reprint

- **Bug:** print fires only **once** per voucher. If the printer was off (very common in the morning) → voucher is saved but no slip prints → screen jumps to the next blank bill.
- Recovery is painful: **Search → reopen bill → edit bill → re-trigger entire print process.**

**Requested fixes**
- A **Reprint button.**
- A **pre-print connectivity check** — verify printer is connected before sending, warn if off.

---

### A11. Internet / cloud failures at the store (also in Part B)

- POS is **fully cloud-dependent** — no internet, no billing; throws exception errors on retry.
- **Double-billing** is real: when net is slow, the bill button appears unresponsive → staff click twice → duplicate quantity.
- **Rush-hour congestion**: all of Patna's shoppers are on Airtel/Jio simultaneously; during peak even Wi-Fi-backed POS chokes. Lines form, staff cuts handwritten manual bills, re-enters into the system later when net is free.
- POS used to be **non-cloud** in the past; 1–2 stores in his "circle" still run the offline build. The vendor doesn't expose that path; the owner doesn't dare poke configs.

**Mandate**
- **Offline-first POS** with auto-sync the moment internet returns. *"Net chale ya na chale, billing nahi ruke."*

---

### A12. "Application" / permission workflow

- Brief mention — an item arriving from another store triggered an **application/request** ("Detail ji ka application diya hai") that had to be approved + inwarded before selling could begin. Implies a request–approval handshake before a store is officially in possession of stock. (Worth confirming in a follow-up.)

---

### A13. Architecture pain — master vs store data

- One master DB serves all logins.
- Every barcode lookup hits master → that's why every scan has latency and every retry can explode into an exception.
- Architecturally: **store-login should only query store-scoped data.** Reduces server load **and** speeds up every scan.

## PART B · POS System Discussion (single block)

A consolidated read of everything said about the **existing POS software itself**, separate from the store workflow.

**Vendor & history**
- Existing POS is a third-party software (vendor name not stated in the transcript).
- Was **originally offline / on-prem.** Vendor migrated it to a **cloud build** ~1 year ago to support multi-store rollout.
- 1–2 of the owner's "circle" stores still run the offline build, so an offline variant exists in the vendor's codebase — but it's never offered/explained and the owner avoids touching config (*"data bahut hai, kya pata kuch ho jaye"*).

**Architecture observations from the live demo**
- **Every barcode scan = synchronous online lookup against the master DB.** Latency-bound. First scan in a session is slowest while the DB connection establishes; subsequent scans are faster bursts.
- Master DB is **unsegmented** — one store's POS queries records for *all* stores; "kahan kahan stock hai" — entirely unnecessary at counter level.
- **No role-based access** — store user / sub-master / master logins all see the same information set. *"Sabka full access hai."*
- Built originally for "ek-do store ke liye, basically billing ke liye." Multi-store and cloud were retrofitted incrementally without re-architecting.

**Billing flow (what works today)**
1. Scan barcode (slow first time, faster afterwards).
2. Discount field: **manual % entry** (typed each time, no rule lookup).
3. Cash discount field: alternative to %, enter a rupee amount; bill adjusts.
4. Mode of payment: **Cash, GPay, Card** are the only three ever used. "Coupon" and other tiles exist but *"pata nahi kya hai, aaj tak use nahi hua."*

**Wallet / loyalty bug**
- Wallet screen throws **"Invalid message text"** and asks for an **OTP** that never arrives.
- Vendor knows; says "you tell us finally if you want it or not"; stuck in "baat-chit phase" for years.
- Customers told "talks are going on" — some sitting on 20–30k unredeemed points.

**Internet dependency / latency failures**
- POS will **not bill** without internet — throws exception errors immediately.
- **Latency alone (without disconnect) also throws errors**, because each operation depends on DB fetch.
- **Double-billing bug**: under slow net the bill button appears unresponsive → staff click again → duplicate quantity posted.
- Rush hour amplifies this: cell network choke even over Wi-Fi → lines form → one staffer cuts handwritten paper bills → re-entered later.

**Master-DB scan per barcode**
- Slow under good net, exception-throwing under bad net.
- Owner's quote: *"Pipeline mein hi dikkat hai, data pipeline mein hi dikkat hai."*
- Race condition: rescan same barcode before first lookup finishes → **Unhandled Exception** popup.

**Print / reprint**
- One-shot print only. After printing, screen jumps to a new blank voucher.
- If the printer was off (common in morning), bill is saved but no slip exists → must Search → reopen → edit → re-trigger print, an entire re-cycle.
- No pre-print connectivity check.

**UI / UX issues**
- Cursor lands in the wrong field after qty entry (transfer flow).
- Long lists scroll the active SKU out of view; no per-SKU running tally visible.
- "Enter city" popup appears where qty was expected.
- No visual loading indicator → staff click twice thinking nothing happened.

**Access control**
- Zero role separation. Every login is effectively admin. Store staff can see master-level data they have no use for; also bloats every fetch.

### Owner's stated requirements for the replacement POS

| # | Requirement | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Offline-first** with auto-sync when net returns (half-hour intervals, or instantly on reconnect) | Net dependency kills billing during rush hour |
| 2 | **UI / layout near-identical to the current POS** | Staff don't have to re-learn. Priyo Ji's explicit concern: *"Changes hum log jhel lein, inko jhelna na pade."* |
| 3 | **Per-store data scope** — only fetch this store's records, not master DB | Speed + reduced server load + no irrelevant data |
| 4 | **No master-DB hit per scan** — local barcode lookup | Eliminates per-scan latency and exception-on-retry |
| 5 | **Reprint button**, plus **pre-print printer-connectivity check** | Daily reality of staff forgetting to switch printer on |
| 6 | **Auto-populated max discount per SKU**, driven by EOSS / brand rules | Removes counter-level decisioning; *"store mein dekhna nahi padega kitna discount"* |
| 7 | **Working points redemption** with a defined ratio (e.g. 5pts=₹1 or 1,000pts=₹500 off), redeemable at bill end | Feel-good factor for regulars; current system is broken |
| 8 | **Visual loading / buffering indicator** during any wait | Prevents double-click → double-bill |
| 9 | **Role-based access** — store user / sub-master / master see different scopes | Currently everyone has full access |
| 10 | **No "useless" tiles/fields** (e.g. Coupon, mystery payment modes) | Keep it lean: Cash / GPay / Card |
| 11 | **Robust handling of rapid repeat scans** (no Unhandled Exception) | Race condition crashes today |

**Owner's bottom line:** *"Software to almost broken hai."* He is explicitly open to the new POS being a **near-pixel clone** of the current UI so staff face zero learning curve, with all the fixes underneath.
