Loom

A fashion-retail ERP should feel like the brands it sells: light, warm, and put together. Loom pairs a frosted glass sidebar floating over a soft pastel wash with a clean white working canvas, and replaces heavy black buttons with muted terracotta and pastel tints. Color is spent on meaning - statuses, brands, alerts - never on decoration that competes with the numbers. Toggle dark mode from the header; every token below has a dark counterpart.

01

Principles

Distilled from current dashboard and ERP design research, filtered through what a 50-store Indian retail chain actually needs day to day.

1

Calm canvas, colorful meaning

The working surface stays white/neutral so dense tables and forms stay readable. Pastel color appears only where it carries meaning: status, category, alert, brand.

2

Frost frames, never floods

Glass effects live on the frame - sidebar, topbar, toasts, overlays. Never behind data tables or forms, where blur wrecks contrast and scanning speed.

3

Numbers are the heroes

₹ values in tabular numerals, lakh/crore grouping, right-aligned. Voucher numbers in mono. A store person should read a ledger row in one glance.

4

Soft geometry, firm hierarchy

Pill buttons, 12-22px radii, generous padding - friendly like the screenshots you liked. But hierarchy stays strict: one page title, one primary action per screen.

5

Show what matters now

Progressive disclosure: the default view answers today's question (sales, pending reviews, expiring SOR windows); detail lives one click behind.

6

Accessible by default

Every pastel chip pairs a tinted background with a dark ink of the same hue (WCAG AA). Focus rings on everything. Frost always has a solid-color fallback.

Rules of the language

The short contract every screen must honor. If a screen needs to break one, we change the rule here first - same discipline as the architecture doc.

Always
  • Frost on the frame only: sidebar, topbar, toasts, palette.
  • One terracotta primary per screen - the posting/money action.
  • Approve = mint, reject = rose, waiting = butter, everywhere.
  • Money in ₹ lakh/crore, tabular, right-aligned. Vouchers in mono.
  • Pastel chip = tinted bg + same-hue dark ink (AA contrast).
  • Focus ring on every interactive element; 44px targets on touch.
  • Every empty state says what the screen is for and offers a next step.
Never
  • No blur behind tables, forms, or any dense data.
  • No solid black pill buttons; no pure-white text on pastels.
  • No zebra striping; hover highlight only.
  • No second accent color per screen; pastel ≠ decoration.
  • No status meaning expressed by color alone - always dot + word.
  • No floating labels, no placeholder-as-label.
  • No new tokens, radii, or shadows outside this kit.

Research behind the principles: UXPin - Dashboard design principles · Fuselab - Dashboard trends 2026 · Axess Lab - Glassmorphism & accessibility · Orizon - Frosted glass without killing UX · UX Pilot - Glassmorphism best practices
