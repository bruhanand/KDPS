KDPS Operating System · Design Language · v0.2 · round two

Atelier — the look of our stores, on screen

One page holding every piece of the new look: frosted glass chrome around a clean white sheet, fabric-swatch pastels for status, coral as the single loud brand note, and a serif-italic accent that gives page titles a fashion-editorial voice. Every component below maps 1-to-1 to a component that exists in the app today, filled with real KDPS content. New in v0.2: the sidebar frost is locked at 50%, and dark mode is in — flip the switch at the top-right to see every component in either theme. Dark is one token-override block, no component changes, exactly the rule the app already follows.

Round two — what's locked, what's open

Round one locked almost everything: 50% frost on the chrome, coral as the brand note, the money-button rule, pastel icon wells in the sidebar, and the ambient wash as shown. Dark mode is now built in — these are the open points left.

The serif-italic accent — confirm it

"Receive goods" — the one question round one skipped. I've kept it; one word from you drops it everywhere.

Dark: how dark is the backdrop?

Right now it's a deep navy night with the same coral–lavender glows. Darker (near-black, more contrast) or softer (more grey-blue, gentler)?

Dark: the money button

Solid navy reads as "just another dark box" on a dark page, so "Post to ledger" becomes a solid indigo there. Happy, or should it stay near-black with a brighter border?

Dark: chip strength

Statuses become translucent tints of the same fabric colours — quiet, ledger-like. Want them punchier (more saturated) instead?
