1.The build at a glance

The foundation and the inbound money-spine are genuinely built and well-architected — a proper deterministic kernel (money-as-paise, append-only ledgers enforced by DB triggers, a document state-machine, gap-free numbering, and a single balanced posting engine). The selling side and everything downstream of it is not built. Two data-flow issues (Section 3) should be closed before more is layered on.

2.The rules of the process — the data lifecycle

The design rests on a strong constitution (in CONTEXT.md): every business event is a document; documents write append-only ledgers; ledgers are never hand-edited; masters are copied (snapshotted) onto documents; corrections are new reversing events, never edits; AI only reads and suggests. The built kernel enforces the mechanical half of these at the database level. The question you raised — "is the whole flow correct?" — is really about the seams between documents and ledgers. Verdict on the flow. The document → stock → value-GL path is correct, immutable and self-balancing — this is the hard part and it is done right. The weaknesses are all at the money-out edge (vendor/cash never re-join the balanced books) and at the receipt edge (GRN carries no ledger quantity). Both are addressable in a focused hardening pass and are exactly what "get the rules right" should cover.

3.Data-flow correctness findings

Ranked. These are the "is the whole flow correct?" answers — divergences between the locked design and the build, or unresolved rules that will bite once selling is layered on.

F1 · Vendor & cash ledgers bypass the balanced posting engine P0 · fix first

The value GL is a proper double-entry book (Σ of every leg = 0). But the vendor payable and cash ledgers are separate single-entry running-balance tables written directly, never through post_entries. Consequences:

  • A PT inward books both a GL leg Cr VENDOR_PAYABLE and a separate VendorLedgerEntry bill — the same liability lives in two unreconciled places.
  • A vendor payment reduces the vendor ledger and cash ledger, but touches the GL not at all — so GL VENDOR_PAYABLE only ever grows and never clears, and cash never appears in the trial balance. The "self-balancing books" claim holds only for the inbound slice.

Fix: make finledger post through post_entries so vendor/cash/inventory are one double-entry system (payment = Dr VENDOR_PAYABLE / Cr CASH), and stop double-booking the payable. This is the single most important step toward "the whole flow is correct" and is squarely a hardening task.

F2 · GRN posts no ledger quantity — two-step inbound is effectively one-step Rule to lock

The constitution says "GRN posts quantity only; PT posts valuation." In the build the stock ledger is only ever written at PT (kinds are pt_inward/pt_reversal); the GRN records quantities on GrnLine but writes nothing to the append-only ledger. So there is a blind window: goods physically received (and, for branded goods at a store, potentially already sellable after a POS scan) are invisible to on-hand until Patna inwards the PT.

Decision needed: is that acceptable (PT = the single point of truth), or do we need a "received-not-inwarded" quantity view / a GRN quantity leg? This directly affects reconciliation against the POS. Lock the rule before selling is built.

F3 · No audit log — Rule 10 only partially met P1

Every client spec promises "every edit needs a remark — who and why, full audit trail," and Rule 10 requires an actor on every action plus an AI-suggestion-vs-human-decision marker. Today there are created_by/posted_by FKs, but no dedicated append-only audit log capturing who changed what, when, and why (reason code) — especially for mutable bookings and for approvals. The Controls "Audit Trail" screen is a stub.

Fix: a small append-only audit_log (actor, action, entity, before/after, reason, ai_vs_human) written by a shared hook. Needed before the approval/exception layer means anything.

F4 · Masters are mutable, not SCD-2 versioned P1 · mitigated

The design calls for effective-dated (SCD-2) masters where money depends on history (gstin, gst_slab, cost, price, brand-terms). Only GstSlab is date-effective; Brand/Store/GSTIN/Season are edited in place. Mitigated because documents snapshot the commercial model and cost at creation — so history isn't silently rewritten today. But a brand's ownership flip (e.g. the EOSS V-flip) has nowhere to live as a dated fact. Add versioning when the money slices need it (payments/SOR), not before.

F5 · GST is carried but not recognised/posted at inbound CA-gated

An INPUT_GST account exists but the PT value voucher is Dr INVENTORY / Cr VENDOR_PAYABLE at full P-RATE with no GST split leg. Cost is taken as P RATE directly (deliberate — never ÷(1+slab)). Whether any brand embeds GST in P RATE, and when SOR/consignment GST is recognised, are CA rulings already flagged in CONTEXT.md. Build the mechanism; hold the rate/rule as data pending the CA.

F6 · Booking sits outside the document FSM P1 · by design, but

Booking is a living, mutable master (numbered ad-hoc, not via the gap-free series) — a conscious call, because an order legitimately mutates through fulfilment. The risk: its edits are ungoverned (no immutability, no audit — see F3). Fine to keep it a master, but pair it with the audit log and a clear "close/cancel needs a remark" gate.

Also still open (from CONTEXT.md, before live money): SOR/Consignment single GST recognition (F9), the 6-month deemed-supply clock, late-freight-after-PT, sold-before-PT handling, and the HSN/apparel-slab sign-off. All CA/Anand rulings — mechanisms exist, the numbers are pending.

4.Environment readiness on Emergent

Hard blocker for building here. The backend is currently down in this Emergent container: the served Python environment has no Django installed (ModuleNotFoundError: No module named 'django'), and PostgreSQL is not installed (only MongoDB runs here) — yet the whole kernel requires Postgres (append-only triggers, isolation, gap-free numbering; SQLite is explicitly rejected). The frontend (Vite) is up on :3000; the API on :8001 is not.

Building on Emergent going forward is fine, but it needs a one-time Phase 0 bring-up: install PostgreSQL 16 in the container, create the kdps/kdps_dev database, install backend deps into the served venv, run migrations + seed_foundation, wire the frontend's API base URL, and restart supervisor. After that, hot-reload dev works normally. (The Render alpha and the local uv/docker path are unaffected — this is purely about making this container a working dev box.)

5.The phased implementation plan

Your stated priority — harden and stabilise what exists before adding new modules — is honoured: Phases 0–2 are stabilisation; new modules start at Phase 3, in the architecture's own build order (selling → offers → payments → Tally → controls → analytics). Each phase notes FREE (AI writes, human reviews after) vs SUPERVISED (money/ledger/GST — every line read, golden-file tested).

Phase 0Environment bring-up on Emergent · DONE
  • PostgreSQL 15 installed + supervisor-managed (survives restarts); role/db kdps/kdps_dev created; Django + deps installed into the served venv; migrations + seed_foundation + seed_ptmapper applied.
  • Verified end-to-end: login 200, authenticated API returns seeded masters, frontend renders, CORS clean, no console errors. Re-provision in one command: scripts/dev-bootstrap.sh.
  • App: https://app-ops-complete.preview.emergentagent.com · demo logins in memory/test_credentials.md.
Phase 1Harden the BRANDED flow, end-to-end — the "rules of the process" pass · SUPERVISED · non-branded deferred

Scope narrowed per Anand: prove the branded spine correct first. The flow to make watertight: Booking (multi-store, brand↔supplier) → receive (store or warehouse) → brand PT mapped → Patna inward (data becomes "true") → scanned into POS → sold → return/defect per policy. Non-branded interactive PT authoring is a later slice.

  • F1 (P0) — unify vendor/cash into the balanced GL via post_entries; end the double-booked payable; extend the Σ=0 trial balance to cover payments and cash. The books must tie beyond inbound.
  • F2 — lock the GRN-quantity rule (is PT the single truth, or do we need a received-not-inwarded view for the store window?) and implement it.
  • F3 — append-only audit_log + shared write hook (who/why/when/AI-vs-human); back the Controls "Audit Trail" screen.
  • Model gaps vs the real branded flow (lock the rule, then fix):
    • Multi-store booking — today a booking has a single destination_store; Anand's flow has one booking spanning several stores for the same brand. Decide: per-line store on the booking, or allocation-at-inward (the spec's model)? Then implement.
    • Brand ↔ supplier — "one brand can have many suppliers." The data model allows it (Vendor↔Brand M2M) but the booking/UI assumes one; confirm and tidy.
    • Return/defect policy per booking — the commercial model is snapshotted, but the return-window + allowance tracking + 30/15/7 alerts the specs promise aren't built. (Execution of returns is Phase 3; the policy clock starts here.)
  • Tighten the RBAC boundary deferred at alpha; regression + new golden-file tests over the branded money path.
  • Exit: a branded booking → receipt → PT → Patna inward → books-tie is provably correct and fully audited, with the multi-store rule settled; suite green on Postgres.
Phase 2Close the inbound loop end-to-end · SUPERVISED + FREE
  • Store allocation / split (warehouse proposes, Patna confirms per store); damage/quarantine flow; booking close/cancel-with-remark; return-window alerts (30/15/7d) + Correction GR-allowance tracking.
  • Confirm the non-brand MRP formula against a worked KDPS example; resolve the GST-at-inbound decision (F5) with the CA.
  • Exit: a booking can be tracked booked → received → inwarded → allocated → return-window, fully audited.
Phase 3Outbound / Selling spine · SUPERVISED · biggest new module
  • The source-agnostic Sale document (posting logic on the Sale, not the adapter); stock-out posting to the ledger; SOR/consignment liability recognised on the sale.
  • Counter sale, customer exchange (against original bill), store-to-store transfer (hold + receive-scan), defective return, SOR season-end return, the V-flip, EOSS, stock count.
  • No longer depends on an outside POS API (26 Jul 2026) — KDPS builds its own counter, offline-first, proven at one store before any second store follows.
Phase 4Offers rulebook · SUPERVISED
  • The 8 discount types as data; item-level (not bill-level) application; brand-vs-KDPS tagging; combination rules (best brand offer + one store/coupon on top, never two brand offers); AMM do-not-discount list; offer-from-photo drafting; margin-cost reporting per brand.
Phase 5Payments & money-in · SUPERVISED
  • Maker-checker with tiered approval bands + auto-escalation; the safety gate (verified bank/GSTIN, inwarded, SOR-sold); multi-bill settlement + part-payments; 3-rail bank reconciliation (cash/card-minus-fee/UPI); debit/credit notes; bank-account-changed fraud flag.
Phase 6Tally sync · SUPERVISED
  • One-way deterministic voucher export (Tally stays the statutory book), keyed on the gap-free doc-number; a daily reconcile check. Needs Tally/TaxOne access.
Phase 7Controls layer · FREE + SUPERVISED
  • Exception inbox (flag-never-block, routed to the named owner), reconciliations surface, approvals surface, and the audit-trail UI over the Phase-1 log. This makes the "flag, never block" rule visible and actionable.
Phase 8Analytics & AI (edges — read/suggest only) · FREE

AI never writes stock/money.
