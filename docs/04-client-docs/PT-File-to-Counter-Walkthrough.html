KDPS Lifestyle Pvt. Ltd. · How to use the system

From PT File to Billing Counter

A menu-by-menu walkthrough: what each person opens, what they press, and what the system does — from the day goods arrive to the moment the barcode scans at the counter.

Who this is for: the warehouse team at Ranchi, the office at Patna, and the store staff. Every menu name below is exactly what is written on the screen. Nothing here needs any technical knowledge.

1.The journey in one line

Goods arrive → receipt made at Receive Goods → PT file mapped or made at PT Files → checked and Sent to Patna → Patna presses Push into system → stock is official → sent to the store under Transfer (only if it landed at the warehouse) → counter syncs under Till & Sync → barcode scans at Billing.

2.Step 1 — Record the delivery

Menu: Receive Goods → Receive

What to do

  1. Open Receive Goods → Receive.
  2. Press New receipt.
  3. Choose the store or warehouse where the goods actually landed.
  4. Type the invoice number and attach the invoice copy.
  5. Enter what physically arrived, line by line.
  6. Save. The system gives the receipt its own number (the GRN number).

Two kinds of delivery — this choice decides everything after

KindWhat it meansWhere the goods landWho supplies the PT file
BrandedInvoice + barcodes on the pieces + a file from the brandUsually at a storeThe brand sends it; the warehouse maps it
Non-brandedInvoice only, no barcodes, no brand fileAt the warehouseWe make it ourselves in the system
Important: the invoice number is not optional paperwork. In the next step the system asks you to pick this receipt by its invoice number, so the goods and their pricing stay tied to the same arrival.

3.Step 2 — Make the PT file

Menu: Receive Goods → PT Files

This menu only appears for the warehouse and the office. Store staff do not see it, and do not need it.

The page has two tabs. Use the one that matches the delivery.

Tab A — PT File Mapper (branded goods)

  1. Step 1 on screen: pick the received invoice from the drop-down. Only arrivals that carry an invoice and have no PT file yet are listed.
  2. Press Map this invoice.
  3. Step 2 on screen: drop the brand's file into the box. Excel and CSV files are accepted.
  4. If the brand's file does not name the brand or the invoice date, fill those two boxes before dropping the file.
  5. The system converts the brand's columns into the 22 KDPS columns and opens the file.

If there is genuinely no invoice, press Map without an invoice. Use this rarely — the link to the arrival is lost.

Tab B — PT File Making (non-branded goods)

  • The top of the tab lists the arrivals that are still waiting for a PT file.
  • Pick one and the system builds a PT file from that receipt, reading the invoice for you where it can.
  • You then fill in the details the invoice never carried — size, colour tier, season — and price the rows.

Two helper screens on this page

4.Step 3 — Check the file and send it to Patna

Menu: Receive Goods → PT Files → press Open on the file

Every PT file page shows a three-step bar at the top, so you always know where it stands:

1 · Warehouse mappingRanchi is still working on it
2 · Sent to PatnaWaiting with the office
3 · Posted to systemDone — stock is real

What to do on this page

  1. Read the coloured note under the buttons. It tells you plainly whether the file is ready or something is missing.
  2. Press Only rows with blanks to see just the rows that need attention.
  3. Press Edit table to fill them. Empty cells are highlighted.
  4. To fill a whole column at once, use the Fill a whole column bar — pick the column, pick the value, choose "only blank rows" or "all rows", press Apply.
  5. When you correct a value, tick remember beside it. The system learns that correction and applies it to every future file.
  6. Press Save changes.
  7. Press Send to Patna.

Extra buttons on a non-branded (made) file

ButtonWhat it does
P · Premium / M · Medium / E · EconomyPuts the price tier into the colour column on every row that has no colour
Free SizeSets "Free Size" on every row with no size
Price blank rowsWorks out the MRP on unpriced rows: purchase rate + 1.1% transport + the category margin, with GST added on top

A red note appears if any row is still missing a barcode or a season. The file cannot go to Patna until both are on every row.

5.Step 4 — Patna pushes it into the system

Menu: Receive Goods → PT Files → open the file marked Sent to Patna

What the office does

  1. Open the file and look through the table.
  2. If it is wrong, press Send back — it returns to Ranchi for correction.
  3. If it is right, pick the booking from Reconcile to booking when there is one. This is optional.
  4. Press Push into system.

What happens the moment that button is pressed

The system does thisWhy it matters to you
Gives the file its own voucher numberThe permanent reference for this inward, with no gaps in the numbering
Adds the quantity to stock at the receiving locationThe goods now exist in the system's stock
Records every barcode as an item, with its brand, design, colour, size, HSN and MRPThis is what lets the counter recognise a scan later
Records the cost of each piece against its seasonProfit can be worked out later without anyone typing it
Writes the accounting entry and the vendor's billThe books and the stock always agree
Locks the fileA posted record is never edited. A mistake is fixed with Reverse posting, which is a fresh, visible correction
This is the single most important moment in the whole flow. Before this button, the goods are only paperwork. After it, they are stock the company owns and the counter can sell.

6.Step 5 — Send the goods to the store

Menu: Transfer

Skip this whole step when the goods were received at the selling store itself — they are already there. It applies when goods landed at the warehouse.

Menu lineUse it for
Transfer → Send StockSending a set of pieces to one store
Transfer → DistributionSplitting one arrival across many stores in a single grid, instead of raising the same transfer store by store
Transfer → Stock RequestA store asking the warehouse for stock
Transfer → In-TransitWatching what has left but not yet arrived
Transfer → TransfersThe full list, and where the receiving store confirms what came in

The order of events

  1. The warehouse raises the transfer and enters the bus number, courier number or vehicle number.
  2. It is approved, then dispatched. The stock leaves the warehouse and shows under In-Transit.
  3. A printed PT travels with the carton.
  4. The store opens the transfer and records what actually arrived. Anything short or extra is recorded there and then.
  5. The stock lands on that store's shelf in the system.

7.Step 6 — The counter

Menu: Sell → Till & Sync, then Sell → Billing

The billing counter works without internet. To do that, it keeps its own copy of everything it needs on the machine — item names, prices, tax rates, offers, salesmen, quantities. That copy is refreshed by syncing.

What to do

  1. At store opening, open Sell → Till & Sync and press Sync now. This pulls a full fresh copy.
  2. Through the day the counter tops itself up quietly in the background.
  3. Open Sell → Billing and scan. The item name and price come up from the counter's own copy, so it works even if the line is down.
  4. If a new arrival does not scan, go back to Sell → Till & Sync and press Sync now once.

What the counter is given, and what it is never given

Sent to the counterNever sent to the counter
Barcode, brand, design, colour, size, HSN and the MRP on the tagThe cost price of any piece
Quantity of each barcode at this store onlyAny other store's stock or prices
GST slabs, live offers, salesmen, the store's own settingsThe company's margins or purchase rates

A store person can see the selling price of everything and the cost of nothing. This is built into the system, not left to trust.

8.Why a barcode does not scan

A new barcode reaches the counter only when all three of these are true. Check them in this order.

#ConditionWhere to check it
1The PT file has been pushed into the system by PatnaReceive Goods → PT Files — the file must read "Posted to system"
2The stock is at this store — received here, or transferred in and confirmedStock → Stock on Hand, or Transfer → In-Transit if it is still on the way
3The counter has synced since thenSell → Till & Sync, then press Sync now

If all three are true and the piece still does not scan, the barcode column on the PT file was most likely left blank or entered wrongly. Patna can reverse the posting and the warehouse can correct it.
