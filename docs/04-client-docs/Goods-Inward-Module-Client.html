Goods Inward Module

How stock moves from vendor delivery to ready-to-sell at the store — receipt, verification, pricing, barcoding and allocation to stores.

Scope — Version 1: Both branded and non-branded goods inward are built in Version 1. Non-branded vendor records, agents and bookings come in Version 2; a non-branded delivery can still be received and made sellable in Version 1 without a booking.

1.Two types of inward

  • Branded — the delivery has all three: an invoice, a barcode on every item, and a retrievable PT/PDF file from the brand. Goods may arrive in several lots.
  • Non-branded — invoice only. No item barcodes and no brand PT file. The style-code is the only reliable detail.

2.Branded — flow

Two paths, by where the goods land:

Store delivery (most common) — Goods + invoice at store → store operator logs in and receives against the order, marking the quantity that arrived line by line and noting anything short or damaged → presses Done → warehouse person reviews, finalises the GRN and converts the PT → Patna inwards → store scans the items → sellable.
Warehouse delivery — Goods + invoice at warehouse → counted, checked and GRN'd at the warehouse → PT converted → Patna inwards → split and allocated to stores → store scans → sellable.
  • The vendor barcode is kept. No new barcode is made for branded goods.
  • PT conversion turns the brand's own file into the KDPS PT format using fixed per-brand rules, built from each brand's PT format. The brand's price and tax details carry across.
  • A store-delivered shipment can be redistributed from one store to another.

3.Non-branded — flow

  • Upload the invoice (PDF or photo); the system reads it into item lines — style-code, quantity and price.
  • A simple GRN is made for what was received (there is no order to check against).
  • The system shows, in a chat, what it filled and what is missing. Missing details are filled through pick-or-type boxes — size/unit, colour tier, category, season, GST% — drawn from the master lists. Anything not known is asked of the vendor by phone.
  • The system checks the item registry and reuses the item if the same vendor + style-code + size was seen before, instead of creating a duplicate.
  • Output: an editable PT file (Excel) that can be reviewed and corrected before inward.
  • Barcode: if the goods already carry a barcode, it is kept; if not, the barcode is generated by the POS software and the warehouse operator fills the barcode column.
  • Warehouse check → Patna inwards → allocated to stores → sellable.
  • MRP for untagged goods = base price + 1.1% transport + GST + a per-category margin (30–35%, adjustable). The transport, tax and margin are calculated automatically.

4.Goods receipt (GRN)

  • Branded: the order list pre-fills the GRN; the receiver records what physically arrived and notes anything short or damaged, then generates the GRN.
  • Non-branded: a simple receipt of what arrived, with no order to compare against.
  • One delivery can have many GRNs — stock can arrive over several lots.
  • A short or extra quantity is recorded as a difference against the order and tracked to a close-out; it does not stop the goods from moving forward to sale.
  • Over-receipt — items or sizes not on the order — can be received on the same screen, with a remark.
  • Every GRN carries two names, taken from logins: who counted and who finalised.

5.Damage

  • At arrival, goods are counted in. Each piece is not inspected for damage at this stage.
  • Damage is checked at the point of selling, in the store.
  • Damage can still be flagged at arrival if seen; a flagged piece is held as not-sellable. It never stops the count.

6.Item identity and barcode

  • An item is identified by brand + style-code + colour + size. Stock is counted at this level, not at style level alone.
  • One barcode = one item identity. The same barcode covers all pieces of that item; stock is the count under that barcode. Five identical pieces = one barcode, quantity five.
  • Season is recorded on every item. The same style-code in a new season is tracked separately, told apart by the season name.
  • Colour, non-branded: when the invoice gives no colour, a price tier — Premium / Medium / Economy — is put in the colour in place of a real colour.
  • Size, non-branded: when the invoice gives no size, the item is "Free Size."

7.Cost and checks

  • Cost of a piece = landed price, excluding GST — the purchase rate after discounts, plus its share of freight and charges, minus GST. It is fixed at inward and not changed afterwards.
  • Profit is derived — sale price from POS minus this cost — not entered by hand.
  • Warehouse check: the converted PT is auto-checked for blank required fields, tax and HSN; only problems are flagged for the person to fix.
  • Patna inward: GST and margin are auto-checked; clean items are bulk-approved and only flags are reviewed. Inward is done by a person at Patna, and is the point the stock becomes official in the system.

8.Store allocation

  • The warehouse proposes how to split a delivery across stores; Patna confirms and inwards per store. Each store sees only its own stock.
  • Splitting is manual in Version 1.

9.Link with the billing counter

Selling, scanning goods in at the store, and stock-on-hand stay in the POS. The KDPS system feeds it and reads back from it.

DirectionWhat moves
KDPS system → POSItem master + price (barcode, MRP, tax/HSN, brand, season); pending-inward quantity per store; later, offer/EOSS rules.
POS → KDPS systemLine-item sales per bill; stock-on-hand per store; daily collection (cash, UPI, card).

The sales and stock read back are used for reconciliation and reporting. The shared key between both systems is the barcode.
