A few business facts we need confirmed

To finish building the system correctly, we need a few facts that only you can confirm. These are about how your business actually runs — not technical questions.

How to answer: short answers are perfect — a number, a yes/no, or a one-line note next to each. You don't need to write paragraphs. If a question is for your accountant (CA), it is marked ask CA — just pass those along.

1 · Your GST numbers

This decides how stock and tax are recorded — especially when goods move between Bihar and Jharkhand.

  1. How many GST numbers (GSTIN) does KDPS have, and for which states? (We believe one for Bihar and one for Jharkhand — please confirm.)
  2. Is KDPS one company (one PAN) holding both GST numbers, or are Bihar and Jharkhand run as two separate firms (two PANs)?
  3. Which stores and warehouses come under the Bihar number, and which under the Jharkhand number? A simple list is fine.

2 · Moving stock between the two states

  1. Do you ever move stock from a Bihar location to a Jharkhand location, or the reverse (for example, the Patna warehouse to a Jharkhand store)? Roughly how often — daily, monthly, or rarely?
  2. When that movement happens today, what paper do you make — a GST tax bill, a delivery challan, an e-way bill, or nothing?

3 · How each brand deal works

The deal type decides who owns the stock, when money is owed to the brand, and how tax is handled. It drives almost everything, so we need it exactly right.

  1. "25-18-10 / Correction" — is this a separate kind of deal, or just an outright Buy & Sell deal with a return allowance added? (In a separate deal, you buy and own the goods, but the brand lets you return up to a limit, with 25% / 18% margins.) हिंदी: 25-18-10 (Correction) — क्या यह एक अलग तरह का सौदा है (माल आप खरीद कर अपना बना लेते हैं, पर brand एक सीमा तक return की छूट देता है, 25% / 18% margin के साथ), या यह बस Buy & Sell ही है जिसमें थोड़ी return की छूट मिल जाती है?
  2. Is there any brand where, in one season, some goods are outright (you own them) and some are sale-or-return at the same time?
  3. For each brand, we need three facts that are blank in the sheet you sent: the deal type (Outright / Correction / SOR / Consignment), the margin %, and the return window (days). Can someone at KDPS fill these in per brand? We will share a simple form.

4 · Sale-or-return & consignment brands ask CA

  1. For your SOR / consignment brands, when goods arrive at your warehouse, does the brand give you a GST tax bill at that moment, or only a delivery challan (with the tax bill coming later, when the goods actually sell)? Your CA can confirm the exact rule — we just need to know the arrangement, so tax is never counted twice.

5 · Accounting & Tally

  1. Do you keep one Tally company file for both GST numbers, or two separate Tally files (one per state)?
  2. Is Tally still your main book of accounts, or are the accounts being moved into Vyapar / TaxOne? If TaxOne, how is it accessed?
  3. Do you issue GST tax bills to business customers (B2B), and roughly how often?

6 · The store billing software (Ten Software)

  1. Which stores run on Ten Software today?
  2. Who is your contact at Ten Software, and can software read sales/stock data out of it and push prices into it (an API or a data export)? If you can request this from them, it helps us start early — it has lead time.

7 · Goods arriving & bookings

  1. Confirm: sometimes stock comes straight to a store without any booking or order being recorded first. Is that right, and roughly how often does it happen?
  2. When you place a booking/order with a brand, would you like the system to also record the MRP / value at that point, or is the order quantity enough?
