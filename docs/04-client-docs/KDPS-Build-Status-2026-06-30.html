The KDPS System — What's Built So Far

A plain snapshot of where the system stands today. It already runs in any web browser — nothing to install. Below is what your team can use now, and what comes next.

Ready to use todayLive · early access

Getting in & Security

  • Secure login with fine-grained roles. 9 role-based views — owner, store staff, warehouse, finance, admin, etc. Store staff see only their assigned store location. Includes break-glass superuser access.
  • 13-section sidebar navigation. Single URL per screen, profile tile, off-canvas drawer on mobile devices, and Dark Mode support (Light / Dark / System).
  • Global search bar. Top-bar search bar supporting barcode wedge-scanning across items, documents, and customers.

Your backbone records (master data)

  • Stores & warehouses, Brands, Seasons, GST registrations (Bihar & Jharkhand), Users & Roles — view, add and edit, all in one place.
  • Customer master. Integrated customer registry with instant counter typeahead and till history.

Day-to-day documents & Goods Inward

  • Vendor bookings. Record seasonal bookings for each brand.
  • Goods inward (GRN). Receive shipments into stores or warehouses with automatic distinction for branded vs non-branded and tagged vs non-tagged items.
  • Self-improving PT converter. Converts brand PT / invoice files into clean stock data. Automatically remembers human corrections, line-item edits, and bulk fill corrections. Uncertain lines go to a review list.

Transfers, Returns & Stock Control

  • Scan-based transfers. Scan out at dispatch, scan in at receipt, with real-time in-transit stock tracking between stores and warehouses. Prints transfer PT documents automatically.
  • Discrepancy & damaged stock handling. Automatic flagging for short, extra, or damaged receipts. Store reports damage, warehouse confirms into quarantine stock.
  • Approvals inbox & Maker-Checker controls. Senior approval required for transfers, variance tolerances, and stock write-offs. No self-approvals.
  • Physical stock count. Blind stock counting workflow where counters enter physical counts without seeing book stock, producing automated variance reports.

Selling at the Counter (POS & Billing)

  • Scanner-first POS counter room. Dedicated fast billing interface built directly into the system with barcode scanning and audio feedback.
  • Integrated returns & exchanges. Process returns and exchanges directly at the billing counter with money-in-only rules (credit notes retired).
  • Crash-proof counter cart safety. Automatic draft saving survives browser crashes, instant undo stack, and rescan quantity increments.
  • Flexible payment rail. Cash, cards, customer prefill, and instant UPI charge card via mock payment adapter.
  • Counter controls & Receipts. Manager discount dials with limit policies, GST breakup popovers, and bill printing with barcode generation.

Stock & money (the books)

  • Stock ledger & Stock on hand. Every movement and live quantity tracked by item: style, size and colour.
  • Vendor ledger & Cash ledger. Vendor dues with ageing, plus complete cash tracking.
  • In-app Workspace inbox. Notifications and team messages accessible directly inside the system header.
  • Self-balancing books. Stock value and brand dues stay in balance at all times, with SOR and outright brands handled correctly and GST built in.

Coming nextBuilding, one at a time

Built in order so each part is checked and correct before the next begins.

  • Promotions, EOSS, Tally Export & Statutory Connections — scheme management (slabs, BOGO, gifts), EOSS bulk billing, markdown planning, and exporting sales/purchases/GST ledgers directly to Tally.
  • Finance & Vendor Payments — maker-checker payment approvals, bank statement reconciliation, and collections.
  • Morning Brief (AI) — suggest-only daily executive summary (AI suggests, human approves).
  • HRMS & Payroll — face check-in attendance, employee records, sales commission and incentive calculation.
  • Business Intelligence & Analytics — brand / store / season P&L, dead-stock and ageing alerts, demand forecasting, custom report builder.
