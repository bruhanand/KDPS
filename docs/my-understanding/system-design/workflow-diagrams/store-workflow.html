How stock becomes sellable at this store. The source SBU (Ranchi warehouse or a sister store) generates a PT file when it saves its outbound voucher — the pending inward is visible on this store's POS login immediately, before the goods physically arrive. Goods come daily. Staff opens the matching voucher and scans every piece by its individual barcode; Received qty climbs while Pending qty drops. The system permits selling a unit before the inward is keyed in (the goods are already in hand) — in that case SOH shows negative for the SKU and auto-corrects once the inward is completed.

The golden path at the till. Offers are read off physical placards and applied by hand — the POS does not enforce them, which is also where the discount-skimming leak sits: a unit eligible for a larger brand-funded discount can be billed at a smaller one. The POS is fully cloud-dependent — any network outage drops the sale to manual billing, reconciled next day. Print is single-shot: if the printer was off when the bill rang up, the bill saves but the slip does not print, and recovery is a Search → reopen → edit → re-trigger print loop.

The firm rule: exchange only — no money back, no store credit. Swaps are MRP-to-MRP. If the new item is cheaper, the customer takes additional item(s) — down to socks or a handkerchief — to cover the gap; if it is dearer, the customer pays the balance. Because an exchange does not re-check discount history, equal-MRP swaps leave no audit trail and can be used to mask an under-applied discount.

Every way a unit leaves a store that is not a customer sale. Internal stock transfer is MBO-only — EBOs do not move stock internally (that traffic is between the brand and the EBO directly). A transfer is created in the Stock Transfer panel: the destination SBU is selected, each piece is scanned (or entered by style code for brands like Jockey, where the PT file is keyed that way), and the voucher is saved — a PT file is generated and the destination immediately sees the items as today's credit / pending inward on its POS login, before the goods physically move. Physically, the goods then move by bus, confirmed over a WhatsApp group with bus + driver number — system-level transfer tracking is deferred to a later ERP phase. Defective / GR returns split by brand: Madura Fashion brands (Allen Solly, LP, Van Heusen, Peter England) pick up directly from the store; every other brand and non-branded stock is consolidated at the warehouse and processed from there. SOR season-end stock returns to the brand within the unsold allowance.

The end-of-season markdown path. Who funds the discount — the brand or KDPS — depends on whether it is a brand offer or an ageing-stock clearance. Most stores lack the floor space to separate offer stock from fresh arrivals, so the whole store goes on sale. The improved POS flow should apply the active discount from the scanned barcode, capture approvals for overrides, and retain discount and margin history for reporting.
