KDPS Lifestyle · Planning-closure check · 26 June 2026

Does the ERP contain everything it should?

A completeness audit of the KDPS design against three independent yardsticks — ERPNext's full module catalog, India apparel-retail ERP standards, and the client's own asks. 47 capability areas checked; each marked Covered / Partial / Deferred-by-decision / Gap.

Verdict. For its declared scope — a multi-brand India apparel retailer running its back office while Ten Software POS and Tally stay external — the KDPS design is genuinely comprehensive and unusually well-reasoned. Of 39 capability areas, the load-bearing retail-ERP spine (SKU=style×size×colour identity, append-only matrix stock ledger, two-step GRN/PT posting, two-axis commercial models, offers rulebook, returns/EOSS/V-flip, payments with maker≠checker≠payer, two-GSTIN tax routing, balanced posting engine, naming series, audit immutability, RBAC scoping, the PT-file maker, and the recurring brand-report duty) is COVERED with cross-checked evidence across constitution, data-model, posting-catalog, lifecycles, ADRs and a UI map — not hand-waved. The honest caveats are that nothing is built yet (foundation slice 0 is the current target) and five money-critical GST/SOR items consciously await a CA ruling, but those are sequencing and external-input facts, not design holes.

The honest gap list

The only things worth acting on. All medium severity — none block the foundation.

MEDIUM Period close / internal freeze-date gate (period-close-fiscal, PARTIAL)

The append-only/reverse-only kernel stops silent MUTATION of history, but nothing stops INSERTION into an already-reported period. A back-dated Sale, stock adjustment, or retro-linked GRN posted into a month whose brand report or SOR settlement was already sent will silently restate a published number — directly eroding trust in the monthly brand pack and the SOR settlement basis, which are the system's two highest-stakes external outputs. Verified: corpus grep returns no freeze-date / period-lock design, while back-dated NEW documents are an explicitly expected case.

What to do → Add to design now, but cheaply — it is one effective freeze-date checked in post_entries against posting_date, on the same engine that already enforces the no-reposting invariant (ADR-0006). Capture as a one-line foundation/posting-engine requirement so the kernel is built with it, not retrofitted.

MEDIUM Store-lifecycle closure + smart opening-stock seed (store-open-close, GAP)

KDPS actively opens and closes stores, yet store CLOSURE (zero-out all stock + matching Tally closing entries + liquidation/staff-reassign) is designed nowhere and has NO deferral decision — verified: it exists only as client FR-21/FR-22 in the rejected Phase-3 plan, and a grep across the whole canonical corpus returned nothing. Without it a shut store can leave phantom stock on the books and an unbalanced Tally. This is the one true uncovered-and-undeferred item.

What to do → Capture as an explicit open question and fold into the deferred D9 migration/rollout scope (it is the natural home — opening-stock seeding already lives loosely adjacent there). Do NOT add to the foundation; just make the deferral conscious so it stops being a silent hole. The building blocks (store active flag, V-flip/liquidation postings, dead-stock AI for the seed suggestion) already exist and only need stitching when D9 is designed.

MEDIUM Brand statement incentive accrual & claim (reconciliation-brand-statement, PARTIAL)

Statement and DN/CN reconciliation are designed, but earned brand incentives (target bonus, display money, advertisement support) have NO accrual/claim/chase flow — verified: they exist only as one unanswered client question and as a free-text 'target incentive' value among ~6 DN/CN reason codes. This is real money: the DN/CN sheet already holds 13,164 debit notes (~₹24M); unclaimed incentives across 40+ brands are a plausible lakhs-per-year leakage with no systematic chase, exactly the margin-recovery KDPS buys the system for.

What to do → Capture as an open question now and slot the design into the payments/settlement slice (D4 territory) rather than the foundation. It is not a kernel concern, so it is safe to handle at its slice — but flag it explicitly so it does not get lost behind the generic DN/CN reason-code, which only records the leakage, it does not recover it.

MEDIUM E-invoicing (IRN) generation (tax-gst-compliance, PARTIAL)

GST calc/data-model is excellently designed, but IRN/e-invoice GENERATION is self-flagged as 'statutory (>₹5cr PAN-wise) but deferred to later and owned by no module.' B2B sales (customer-GSTIN bills) and cross-state IGST stock-transfer invoices between the two distinct-person GSTINs legally require IRN, and the AATO threshold is trending down toward ₹2cr. With no owning module, those invoices would ship without IRN once the money slices go live — a statutory compliance gap, not just a feature gap.

What to do → Capture as an open question tied to the already-planned CA sitting (which decides e-invoicing applicability). The design correctly notes IRN must be independent of the Tally path. Safe to handle at the money slices — but assign it an owning module now and confirm applicability with the CA before, not during, that slice, so it is not discovered late.

Three cheap things to do before/while building (from the critic):
  1. Bake an internal freeze-date check into the posting engine you're about to build — one date checked in post_entries so a back-dated document can't silently restate an already-sent month. Cheap if built in now, painful to retrofit.
  2. Log store-closure, brand-incentive accrual, and IRN/e-invoicing ownership as conscious open questions — so they stop being silent holes (store-closure → folds into D9 migration; incentive accrual → payments slice; IRN → the CA sitting).
  3. Add one line to the outbound/banking slice spec for customer udhaar (credit sale) so its omission is conscious, not a surprise.

Deferred by decision — choices, not holes

  • Merchandising / OTB / assortment planning — consciously excluded (no budget gate; Rule 5 flag-don't-block; allocation exists only as suggest-only AI). Trigger to revisit recorded: when D7 analytics shows season buys drifting from plan, add a read-only season-buy view (never blocking).
  • Customer / CRM, loyalty, gift cards, memberships, store-credit wallet — out of scope by decision (Ten Software owns the customer at the counter; KDPS ingests the finished sale). Known future offer patterns catalogued but unbuilt with no customer/points/gift-card data model. One residual: the out-of-scope reversal (PRD assumed KDPS would build POS/billing/loyalty) should be confirmed with the client in writing.
  • HR, Attendance & Payroll (WAPCS-style face+GPS, shift/leave rules, incentive engine, payroll-to-Tally) — explicitly deferred by recorded decision; no design folder exists. One loose thread flagged in-doc: whether a minimal 'staff present/absent' capture rides into the store dashboard (OPS-03) is an in/out decision to make before go-live.
  • D9 Migration & Go-Live (masters import, opening-stock = one physical count per store, pilots-then-waves in parallel with Tally, Hindi training videos) — the only design discussion not yet done; deferred by decision, to be designed before go-live after a pilot cluster. Single riskiest event; mitigations on record (no big-bang, parallel Tally run, commercial-model grid flagged as a migration blocker to capture now).
  • Omnichannel / e-commerce — out of scope (no online sales); pre-designed as a seam (online = new Location type + new Sale source). Seam asserted in the design-of-record but not yet exercised in the data-model; verify when/if added.
  • Deep roles/access elaboration (actual role catalog, org hierarchy, ₹ approval band values) — shape is designed and ratified (ADR-0003/0007); the specifics are parked pending client input (CD5).
  • Software-initiated bank payments — v2 behind a swappable bank adapter (manual execution + read-only statement pull now).
  • Manufacturing/BOM/subcontracting, subscriptions/recurring billing, fixed assets/projects/QMS/helpdesk/maintenance, multi-currency — correctly excluded by domain fit (pure INR apparel retailer); their absence is by design, not oversight.

Blind-spots checked (the things you'd fear missing)

Adversarial sweep of the classic ERP traps — mostly already handled.

  • Adversarially checked the classic blind spots and most are actually handled — recording them so the architect can stop worrying: DATA BACKUP/DR is covered (managed Postgres, automated backups + PITR, build-roadmap line 491). NEGATIVE STOCK / OVERSELL is covered and deliberate (flag-don't-block: auto-inward-on-scan prevents it live, EOD recon + HO exception resolves it, persistent negatives are a first-class anomaly flag; block-at-write was consciously rejected because KDPS is not the book of record). CONCURRENCY ON THE SAME SKU is moot by construction (POS owns the till; stock is an append-only signed-delta sum, not a mutable balance, so there is no lost-update race) and the one place atomicity matters — voucher numbering — uses SELECT...FOR UPDATE / sequence-per-prefix. REFUND-TO-ORIGINAL-TENDER / GIFT-CREDIT-NOTES are covered (exchange money-gap posts as a tender leg into the day's collection; vendor DN/CN netting is designed). STORE CASH FLOAT / PETTY CASH is covered (opening float in OPS-03, petty-cash deduction on the cash rail, three-rail audit). PRICE-CHANGE APPROVAL rides the maker-checker on the 'dangerous four' masters (GST slabs, brand terms) plus offer approval limits. CUSTOMER PII is minimal-by-design (POS owns the customer; KDPS holds no customer master — which is also why loyalty is correctly deferred). AUDIT-LOG RETENTION: who/why/when is mandated on every row and ledgers are never hard-deleted, though an explicit retention/archival horizon is not stated (low risk, single-tenant).
  • The only genuinely thin adversarial item that maps to a finding above is GST RETURN FILING (GSTR-1/3B) — like e-invoicing it is delegated to Tally as the statutory book, but the IRN piece is the one with no owning module; that is captured in the tax-gst-compliance gap.
  • UDHAAR / customer credit-sale (buy-on-tab where the customer owes money) is noted in the banking lens as not modeled as its own cash-ledger leg — low impact for cash-and-carry apparel, but worth one line in the outbound/banking slice spec so it is a conscious omission rather than a surprise.
Bottom line. Yes — it is safe to close planning and start building the kernel; the spine is thoroughly and consistently designed for KDPS's scope, provided you do three cheap things first: bake an internal freeze-date check into the posting engine you're about to build, and log store-closure, brand-incentive accrual, and IRN/e-invoicing ownership as conscious open questions (none block the foundation) so they stop being silent holes.
