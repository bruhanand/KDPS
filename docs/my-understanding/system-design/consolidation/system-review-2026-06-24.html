System Design Review & Document Sync Audit

24 June 2026 · A reconciliation pass over the whole design record — what is sound, where the documents have drifted from each other, the three new scope items, and the questions to settle before any code is scaffolded.

1 · Executive summary & system verdict

The KDPS ERP design is well-conceived and largely build-ready: a small stable kernel (documents write ledgers; ledgers are derived and never hand-edited; corrections are reversing entries) wrapped around bought edges (Ten Software POS, Tally), with SKU = Brand × Style × Colour × Size keyed on barcode+season, four first-class commercial models (Outright, Correction, SOR, Consignment), GST modelled as date-effective data, and Tally kept as the statutory book of record. The module designs D1–D8 are locked and internally coherent, and the PT-file column set, the 8-pattern offer engine, the DN/CN flow, and the two-GSTIN geography all match the real client data. The build is blocked not by missing design but by a few load-bearing contradictions that corrupt the books if shipped as-is and by four buildable artifacts that do not yet exist (a glossary/CONTEXT, an ERD, a posting catalog, and a consolidated state-machine reference). The single newest doc — the consolidated design-of-record (untracked) — is also the largest source of drift: it still says TypeScript end-to-end and PostgreSQL row-level security, both contradicting later/other decisions. Verified India retail-ERP research confirms the design's instincts are right (two-step receipt, ITC is a property of the matched invoice not the GRN, cross-state transfer is a taxable IGST supply, B2C apparel till needs no per-sale IRN) while correcting several stale GST facts (apparel is now 5% up to ₹2,500 and 18% above, effective 22 Sep 2025; intra-state e-way bill threshold is ₹1,00,000 in both Bihar and Jharkhand). The documentation needs a deliberate reconciliation pass and a single source-of-truth hierarchy before any code is scaffolded.

System verdict — designed in the right direction. The solid core is genuine and should not be re-litigated: documents-write-ledgers with reversing-entry corrections, SKU identity = barcode+season carried end-to-end, the four commercial models as a first-class dimension, vendor-liability timing by model, profitability derived (PT cost × POS revenue) never typed, snapshot masters, flag-don't-block, AI at the edges (read-only, suggests-never-sets), Tally as statutory book fed one-way with a KDPS-owned deterministic voucher number, and a modular monolith over one Postgres. Verified external research validates these as standard India retail-ERP best practice.

The real risks are four contradictions that touch money or statute and must be settled before coding:

  1. GRN-vs-PT stock-ledger writer — answered three different ways across ARCH, CONS and D2;
  2. the over-claimed one-line principle that "PT computes vendor liability" — true only for Outright/Correction; SOR accrues on sale, Consignment never from the PT;
  3. cross-state inter-store transfer booked as "internal — none" in D3 while D6 correctly calls it a taxable IGST supply (a real statutory bug, and D6 itself is internally split on it);
  4. the design-of-record carrying a stale engine-language and data-scoping decision.

None of these is a redesign — each is a wording/posting decision plus a doc edit. The build should proceed once the four buildable artifacts exist (glossary, ERD, posting catalog, state-machine reference) and the two un-ratified stack decisions (engine language, RLS-vs-queryset) are consciously made rather than silently inherited from the distrusted foundation artifacts in the other checkout.
