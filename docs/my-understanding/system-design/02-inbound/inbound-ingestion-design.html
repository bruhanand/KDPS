System Boundary Locked

This is the single most important framing — it decides what we build and what we don't.

v1 scope (corrected 10 June): both inbound paths build in v1. Q35
  • In v1 (core): the non-branded inbound flow — AI invoice reading, the PT chat assistant, P/M/E tiers, Free Size, the MRP formula, barcode generation. This is the deal's PT Mapper Type 2.
  • Waits for its own discussion: non-branded vendor management — vendor records with terms, agents, bookings (parked in the D1 doc §13).
  • So one consequence: a v1 non-branded shipment may arrive without a booking. Its Shipment + GRN stand alone, with a minimal vendor record (name + city) made on the fly — and no ordered-vs-received check until non-branded booking lands.

What our system owns

All inbound truth — the source of truth up to the moment stock is allocated to a store:

  • Invoice capture, GRN, PT files & conversion
  • Validation & head-office inwarding
  • The per-store allocation

What POS owns (external app, talks via API)

We are not rebuilding POS — we feed it and read from it.

  • POS handles: scan-to-receive at the store, selling, provisional-sell, and stock-on-hand (including any temporary negatives).
  • We push: the per-store allocation to POS.
  • We pull: sales back — for reconciliation and (later) forecasting.
POS = tensoftware.in — online-first, so a REST API is likely. Full push/pull contract in the POS Integration section. Two load-bearing answers to confirm with them:
  • Does the API accept a pending-inward / incoming-transfer quantity?
  • Can it export line-item sales back to us?
Join key both sides agree on: the barcode.
Dependency on the Booking chat: GRN is generated against the order list. This chat assumes a structured order list is available to pre-fill the GRN; booking itself is designed elsewhere.

Central Data Model Locked

The Shipment is the anchor Q22 · Q33

One vendor arrival = one Shipment (renamed from "Consignment" to avoid clashing with the Consignment commercial model). Created the moment an invoice is uploaded. The invoice, GRN, PT, store-split, and TIR export all hang off the Shipment. Invoice is the trigger; GRN is a step — neither is the parent.

Status: Shipment + line-item Q10

The Shipment has one headline status for the work queue. Underneath, each line item carries its own state, because shortages, damages, and store-splits are per-item. A Shipment is "done" only when all its lines resolve.

GRN granularity = source-driven Q2

Branded = style-code + size. Non-branded = style-code + size + qty (size missing → "Free Size"). We count at whatever grain the source provides, but size is always present.

One barcode → many items Q41 · changed

New client truth: across the whole database one barcode can carry many physical pieces — the barcode is the identity (a product), not a serial number for one piece. A product's identity = brand + style-code + colour + size; stock is a count of pieces under that barcode per location. This reverses the earlier "one barcode per piece." Happens mostly for non-branded — where colour collapses to a P/M/E tier and size to "Free Size," so many pieces share one identity — but for branded too.

SKU & Barcode Identity Locked

Three different things people often blur together — keeping them separate is what makes stock, reorder, and dead-stock work.

SKU = the sellable identity (the "type") Q31

Key = Brand + Style-code + Colour + Size — and this identity is the barcode. It's the type; pieces are not serialised, they are a count under the identity. For non-branded, "Brand" = the vendor and "Colour" = the P/M/E tier. MRP and season are attributes with history, not identity. The same style next season reuses this identity (for reorder & carryover); each season is a distinct stock cohort keyed by barcode + season (resolved in D3/outbound).

Barcode = the ID of a product identity, shared by its pieces Q31 · Q41 · changed

One barcode covers many pieces — 5 identical kurtis share one barcode and count as quantity 5 (this reverses the earlier "5 pieces → 5 barcodes"). The barcode is the identity (brand + style + colour + size). Within a season the barcode is unique to that identity; some brands reuse a barcode value in a later season, so the full key is barcode + season. Non-branded: KDPS generates one barcode per identity. Branded: keeps the vendor barcode.

Piece = one unit of an identity; stock is a count Q32 · Q42

Pieces of one identity are not individually barcoded — stock rolls up as a count per identity (barcode) per location. Damage is not checked at arrival: goods are counted in, and the damaged/quarantine check happens later, at selling in the store. The arrival damage-flag option stays available but never blocks the count (Rule 5).

Season is load-bearing — D8 critical path. Three locked pieces lean on the season value: the booking, the PT (SEASON column), and stock identity itself (unit key = barcode + season) — yet no module owns the season master until D8. Interim rule Q39: seasons are never free text. The system derives the next season code from the fixed pattern (season family + month, as in the PT Master Sheet) and presents it at PT-creation; the WH person — who makes the PT, Patna does not — selects it. D8 must still settle: the season open/close ritual, who may correct a wrong season, and cross-season barcode reuse. Flagged as a critical-path input to D8.

Identity reuse across arrivals

Reuse rolls a new arrival into the existing identity / barcode and adds to its count — it does not mint a duplicate. Branded: same style+size+colour rolls up to the same SKU across arrivals (enables sell-through & reorder). Non-branded: central registry + deterministic reuse on vendor + style-code + size (see Non-Branded PT).

Branded PT Locked

What counts as branded · where the GRN is made Q30

Branded when: there is an invoice · every item carries a vendor/brand barcode · a retrievable PDF/PT exists (it may arrive in several lots — "together" is not the test). Path A (store delivery, most common): the store operator logs in and receives against the order — marks what actually arrived, presses Done — then the warehouse person reviews, finalises the GRN and converts the PT; the shipment may then be redistributed store → store Q38. Path B (warehouse delivery, branded-by-treatment): counted and GRN'd at the warehouse.

Conversion = deterministic software SW Q1·Q5

Brand PT files are Excel with fixed per-brand formats. Conversion uses per-brand mapping rules built at dev time (developer owns them, from formats the client provides). No AI on the happy path — fast, free, identical every time, fully auditable.

Unknown / changed format → park & alert Q6

If a file doesn't match a known mapping (new brand, or brand changed format), it parks in an "unmapped" queue and alerts the developer to add the rule. Never a risky auto-convert.

Happy path is silent; failures can use chat Q27

Branded conversion is automatic and invisible. Only failures may drop into a chat-style review view (same idea as non-branded). The normal path adds no clicks.

Non-Branded PT Locked · core v1

Build status: v1 — core scope (the deal's PT Mapper Type 2) Q35. In v1 these shipments may arrive without a booking (non-branded vendor management is parked — D1 §13): the vendor is a minimal record (name + city) created on the fly, and the GRN stands alone with no order list to compare against.

Chat / agent UX, output-driven AI Q26·Q27

1) Upload invoice PDF/image → AI reads it. 2) AI replies in chat with a structured report — what it filled vs what's missing. 3) User supplies missing data through type-and-select boxes, not free text — every field with a master list (size / unit, colour P/M/E, category, season, GST%…) opens a pick-or-type control drawn from the Master Sheet, so entries stay standard (anything not on hand is asked of the vendor by call). 4) Output = an editable Excel PT the user can download, view, and hand-edit for anything still missing.

Required-field checklist = the PT format itself Q4

The fixed PT format is the checklist; any blank required column triggers the "ask vendor" prompt. Real columns (OWNER Work Sheet): SEASON · BRAND · COLOR · GENDER · SUB CATEGORY · TYPE · ITEM · FIT · SIZE · BARCODE · DESIGN · HSN · QTY · MRP · BASIC · P RATE · INPUT TAX · OUTPUT TAX · NAG · MARGIN · SUGGESTED SUB CATEGORY · SUGGESTED TYPE. The Master Sheet supplies the dropdown lists (seasons, brands, colours, categories, sizes, GST%).

Colour = real colour, or a P/M/E tier Q28

The COLOR column holds a real colour when one is available — from a brand PDF/PT or stated on the invoice. Only when no colour is given (pure non-branded, invoice silent) does the operator pick a price tier — Premium / Medium / Economy — in the chat, by their own judgment. No fixed price bands; the operator decides. (Resolved 17 Jun: use the invoice's colour if it states one, else P/M/E.)

Size — given, or "Free Size" Q29

If the invoice states size, use it. If size is missing, the line is "Free Size" — we do not go item-by-item guessing sizes. Size is always present on the SKU, never dropped.

Pricing when untagged Q7

MRP = base + 1.1% transport + GST + per-category default margin (30–35%), person can override within the band. Transport %, GST, and the formula auto-calculate.

Barcode — keep it, or generate then fill the column Q8 · Q41

Two cases:

  • Barcode already present: keep it — the PT is built with every item carrying its barcode, then straight to Patna inward and store allocation.
  • No barcode: the PT is created editable with the barcode column empty; once everything else is filled in order, the POS (Ten Software) generates the code and the warehouse operator fills the barcode column, then on to Patna inward and allocation.

One barcode is generated per identity, not per piece (Q41). Barcode generation today is done by the POS — we integrate that; native generation can come later.

SKU policy: registry + reuse — follows the PRD Q16 · changed

The PRD asks for a central SKU registry + reuse (its named gap was "same product → multiple SKUs, no reuse mechanism"). So we reverse the earlier "duplicates fine."

  • Deterministic reuse (now): key = vendor + style-code + size. Seen before → roll up to the existing SKU (type) / barcode and add to its count; don't mint a new identity, and don't create a second barcode for the same identity (Q41).
  • Cross-style similarity (later, suggest-only): same product under a different style-code is hard with thin invoices — AI suggests a match, handler confirms; never an automatic merge.

GRN & Discrepancy Locked

GRN generated against the order list Q24-context Booking dep.

Order list pre-fills expected items + qty → person checks what was physically received → marks shortage/damage → presses Generate GRN. It's a confirm-and-adjust screen, not data entry.

Non-branded GRN = a simple receipt Q43

When there is no order to check against (non-branded, often booking-less — Q35), the GRN just counts what physically arrived from the invoice + goods — no ordered-vs-received comparison. It still exists as a record (you need it for the PT step and the audit trail), and the over-receipt / damage handling below still applies.

Provenance: counted-by ≠ entered-by Q38

The GRN carries two separate actors, both auto-stamped from logins — never typed. Store delivery: the store operator logs in, sees the order in their own queue, records received-vs-ordered and presses Done — they are counted by. The WH person who reviews, finalises the GRN and converts the PT is entered / approved by. Warehouse delivery: the WH receiver is both. No GRN exists without both names on it (Rule 10) — when a discrepancy surfaces weeks later, accountability lands on a person, not a building.

Discrepancy = soft flag, never blocks Q3

Discrepancy is ordered vs received. Goods are physically in and must sell — so the flow continues, the discrepancy is logged and tracked, resolved in parallel.

Over-receipt = same screen, add a line Q32

Items or sizes that weren't booked can still be received — the receiver adds a line on the same screen, flagged as a variance with a required remark. The original order line is kept, flagged. One order → many GRNs as stock arrives over several shipments.

Arrival counts; damage is caught at selling Q32 · Q42

At arrival the goods are just counted — we do not hold up receiving to inspect each piece for damage. The damaged/quarantine check happens later, at selling in the store. The arrival damage-flag option stays available (added, not removed) but never blocks the count (Rule 5).

If damage is spotted at arrival, the old handling still applies: the piece is counted as received, enters stock quarantined (not sellable), and routes to the defective-return flow — never silently dropped.

Account manager owns closing it Q24

Records a structured outcome against the Shipment line — credit note expected / brand acknowledged / write-off — and it stays open until explicitly closed. Feeds credit-note tracking later. Every state change logged.

Validation Gates Locked

Both human gates use the same pattern: auto-validate, flag exceptions only, human approves for accountability.

Warehouse approval (before Patna) Q25

System auto-checks the converted PT — all required fields filled, taxonomy mapped, GST/HSN present, no blanks — and flags only problems. Person approves clean ones, fixes flagged. Gate kept (don't skip), because non-branded PTs have manual fields that can be wrong.

Patna inward (GST + margin check) Q19

System auto-validates GST (vs HSN) and margin (vs expected band), flags only exceptions; Patna bulk-approves the clean ones, reviews only flags. The inward action stays with Patna so accountability is human.

Cost & Posting Spec Locked

The biggest gap in the first version of this design: the PT priced items but never said what a piece costs us or what it writes into the books. Fixed here. This section is the wire into the D4 payments module.

Unit cost at stock-in — the canonical COST Q36

Cost of a piece = landed cost, ex-GST. Built in three steps:

  • Start with the per-piece payable to the vendor after all line-level discounts. In the real sample PT this is P RATE (exactly MRP − margin%; Status Quo: ₹1,649 × 0.78 = ₹1,286.22). The per-brand mapping declares which column carries it — and whether GST is inside it and must be stripped.
  • Remove GST. Input GST comes back as credit, so it is never cost.
  • Spread invoice-level amounts: add this piece's share of invoice-level charges (freight, handling), subtract its share of invoice-level trade discount — both spread across lines in proportion to line value, never by piece count.

The result is stamped on the stock-in posting and frozen (Rule 3 snapshot) — later master or rate changes never rewrite it. Profitability reads it (POS revenue − this cost); aging and dead-stock value with it. One definition for all four commercial models — for SOR and Consignment it is simply the settlement rate (MRP − margin%), ex-GST.

Why ex-GST: counting recoverable GST overstates cost and makes every margin report read worse than reality. Why value-weighted spreading: freight follows value, and the rule never needs re-arguing per shipment.

What the PT posts, per commercial model Q37

Every posting fires at Patna inward — the one moment the data becomes official (TIR already fires there, Q20). Before that, a GRN is physical custody only, not books. The commercial model is read from the snapshot on the booking (Rule 3), never from the live brand master.

Store Split Now: manual Later: AI

Warehouse proposes the per-store allocation; Patna confirms + inwards per-store PT, so each store sees only its own allocation.

Now — manual, but capture training data Q23

Person splits manually. Capture per-line-item allocations (style/size → store → qty, tied to Shipment, brand, season) as structured rows from day one. This is the future training set.

Later — AI forecasting suggests the split

Stage 1 (cold start): fully manual, system records every split. Stage 2: AI forecasting models suggest the split from sales history; person adjusts. Stage 3: forecasting sharpens with more data. POS sales import is a later phase (clean & standardize data first, then feed forecasting). KDPS has sales data per store and per brand, just not import-ready at launch.

Cross-Cutting Rules Locked

Invoice handling AI Q11·Q21

Invoice is a PDF/image. AI reads it into structured data. Verification is risk-based: non-branded (invoice becomes the product + pricing) must be confirmed; branded (invoice only cross-checks) trusts directly with light review. Original file is kept permanently in object storage, linked to its Shipment (GST audit needs ~6–8 years minimum).

Notifications Q9

In-app is the system of record + work queue (auditable, a real queue people act on). WhatsApp is the nudge — deep-links into the app. WhatsApp is never the source of truth.

Access control Q12

Fixed roles (Store, Warehouse, Patna/HO, Admin) + data scoping: a store sees only its own Shipments; warehouse and Patna are network-wide; admin sees all + config.

Reconciliation SW+ narrow AI Q17·Q18

Scope here = Invoice ↔ GRN ↔ PT. Runs EOD batch only (client's demand). Mechanical quantity/value matching = strong algo; AI only to decide whether two differently-worded line items are the same product.

TIR export SW Q20

Fires after Patna inwards (data is official). Pure algorithm, no AI — accounting export must be exact and reproducible. If recon later finds an issue, it raises a correction, not a block.

Audit trail — automatic

A byproduct of event-by-event design: every action writes an immutable record (who, what, when, value before → after). Nobody "writes" the audit trail. Build this way from day one and audit is complete and free.

Glossary

PT file
The main KDPS document listing every item, its price, and tax details. Built per brand in a fixed format.
GRN (Goods Receipt Note)
Record of what was physically received, generated against the order list.
Shipment
The anchor record for one vendor arrival — invoice, GRN, PT, split, and TIR all link to it. (Renamed from "Consignment" to avoid clashing with the Consignment commercial model.)
SKU
The sellable identity (the "type"): Brand + Style-code + Colour + Size — carried by one barcode. Stock is counted per SKU / barcode per location.
Barcode
The ID of a product identity, shared by all its pieces — 5 identical kurtis share one barcode (quantity 5), not five barcodes (changed; was per-piece). Unique to the identity within a season; some brands reuse a barcode in a later season, so the unit key is barcode + season. Branded keeps the vendor barcode; non-branded gets one KDPS barcode per identity.
P / M / E
Premium / Medium / Economy — a price tier the operator puts in the COLOR column for non-branded items that have no real colour.
Free Size
The size value used for a non-branded line when the invoice gives no size.
TIR
The strict, accounting-ready dataset/format the accounting system ingests. Generated by software after Patna inward.
Branded vs Non-branded
Counts as branded when all three exist: every item barcoded, a detailed invoice, and a retrievable vendor PT file. Otherwise non-branded.
Patna (HO)
Head office; checks GST + margin and inwards the PT into the system.
Store split / allocation
Dividing a warehouse Shipment across stores by what each store needs; inwarded per-store so each store sees only its allocation.
Commercial models (4)
Outright (Buy & Sell — KDPS owns, no return) · Correction (KDPS owns, capped 25-18-10 return allowance on delivered qty) · SOR (brand owns, all unsold returns, no cap, fresh booking to reorder) · Consignment (brand owns, like SOR + rolling top-up). See D1 §3. The 25-18-10 belongs to Correction, not SOR (corrected 13 Jun Q&A).
