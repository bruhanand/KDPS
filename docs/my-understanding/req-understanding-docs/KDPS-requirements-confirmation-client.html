1.The big picture

  • We are building one central system for KDPS covering: stock from brand to store, PT file making, stock aging, daily cash & payment audit, and brand reports.
  • Billing in stores moves to our own counter, built by us and rolled out one store at a time (decided 26 July 2026). Until a store is switched, staff keep billing exactly as they do today.
  • Tally remains your account book. Our system prepares and sends entries to Tally; it never replaces it.
  • Non-branded purchases are a core part of the system: photo/PDF of the vendor's invoice → the system reads it → a ready KDPS PT file with barcodes. (The "PT Mapper" — both kinds: brand PT files and plain invoices.)
  • One sitting is still needed on non-branded ordering & vendor records (agents, verbal orders, vendors without GSTIN) — how that information should enter the system. Receiving and PT-making for non-branded goods is not waiting for that.
  • Owners will get key numbers on WhatsApp daily; staff will use the system in a browser on store computers — no app installs.
  • A central sales dashboard is included — every store's sales in one place, one login. (Confirmed 11 June.)

2.Brands & buying terms

The three ways KDPS buys

  • SOR (25-18-10): brand supplies stock. KDPS keeps 25% on full-price sales, 18% during discount/EOSS sales, and may return up to 10% of the season's goods.
  • Outright (Buy & Sell): KDPS buys and owns the stock. No returns. Two kinds: goods with the brand's barcode (MRP fixed by brand) and goods without (KDPS sets MRP).
  • Consignment (2–3 vendors only): vendor's goods sit in KDPS stores; sold is sold, vendor takes back the rest himself.
  • A brand has one usual model, but an individual order can occasionally be on a different model.

Non-branded purchases (local / agent vendors)

  • Non-branded goods arrive at the warehouse only, with an invoice and no barcodes; the only reliable code on them is the style number.
  • The system reads the invoice, asks your person for whatever is missing, and produces the KDPS PT + barcode stickers.
  • When the invoice gives no size, the item is recorded as Free Size.
  • One agent per city introduces non-branded vendors and stays answerable until delivery. (Confirmed.)
  • MRP for untagged goods today = cost + ~1.1% transport + GST + 30–35% margin by category. Correct?
  • Do all your non-branded vendors have a GSTIN? (Why we ask: buying from a vendor without one is booked differently in the accounts, and payments need the GSTIN on file — so we have to know if such vendors exist.)

Questions on terms

  • The 10% return allowance — calculated on what value? The billed value of the season's purchases? Including GST or without GST? Does every SOR brand calculate it the same way?
  • The return window (60–120 days) — counted from which date? Goods receipt? Season end? Brand's invoice date?
  • When an SOR brand delivers goods, do they give a tax invoice immediately, or a delivery challan with the invoice coming later? (Please ask your CA — it decides how Tally entries are made.)
  • Are the two margins (25% / 18%) the same for every SOR brand, or do some brands have different percentages?
  • Do any brands give extra incentives — target bonus, display money, advertisement support? Should the system track them?

Data needed

  • Brand list — all 40+ brands with, for each: buying model (SOR / Outright / Consignment), margin %, return window days, payment terms (credit days).
  • Per-brand PT file format — one sample PT Excel from every brand (the file they send with goods).
  • For each brand's PT: which column is the rate KDPS actually pays, and is GST included in that column?

3.Placing orders (booking)

  • Orders are placed in person at the brand; the brand gives a printed "receiving" paper listing styles, sizes, quantities.
  • In the system: photo/upload of that paper → order is drafted automatically → your person checks and confirms. The order exists in the system from day one, not when goods arrive.
  • An order is style + size + quantity. No colour, no prices at this stage — prices come with the goods (PT). Correct?
  • One order belongs to one brand and one season; goods may arrive in several lots against it.
  • The system will always show ordered vs received, and chase what hasn't come.
  • Do brands ever part-cancel an order from their side? How do you come to know?

4.Goods arrival

What we understood

  • Branded goods arrive at the store, with an invoice; non-branded goods come to the warehouse. Every branded piece carries the brand's barcode.
  • The same style in a new season is kept separate, season-wise — and a barcode repeated in a later season is also separated by season.
  • When goods land at a store: store staff will open the order on their phone, mark what actually came, and press Done. The warehouse person then completes the entry.
  • When goods land at the warehouse: counting and entry happen there.
  • Short supply / excess supply / damaged pieces happen. The system never stops work for this — goods keep moving, the difference is recorded and chased separately.
  • Damaged-on-arrival pieces are kept aside (not sellable) and go back to the brand.
  • Nothing becomes sellable until Patna (head office) approves — same as today, just on screen instead of paper.
  • The warehouse proposes which store gets how much; Patna confirms. Each store sees only its own share.

Questions

  • Who decides the store-wise division today, and on what basis (store size, past sales, manager demand)?
  • Transport on purchases — who pays, KDPS or the brand? If KDPS: per shipment or monthly?

Data needed

  • Season list — the season names/codes KDPS uses (e.g. SS26, AW26) and when each opens/closes.
  • The current barcode tool — which software/machine prints barcodes today (name, a sample sticker).

  • Would stores accept a fixed counting rhythm (for example, one section every week, so the full store is covered monthly)?

9.Offers

What we understood (from the 1 June discussion)

  • Offers are brand-specific and change often. Types seen so far: value slabs (e.g. buy for ₹3,000 get X off), Buy 2 Get 1 (cheapest free), gift above an amount (e.g. a bag), and combinations.
  • An offer can apply to some stores only, and has start/end dates — with a fallback offer after it ends.
  • The system will keep every offer on record, so bills can be checked against the offer later (as we do in the monthly reports).
  • Who creates and approves an offer today, and how does it reach the stores (WhatsApp message? phone call?)
  • If two offers could apply to one bill, what wins — better for customer, or brand's instruction?
  • Gift items (bags etc.) — are they in stock records today, or outside?
  • Every offer circular/image as it comes from a brand — ongoing (like the FM offer list already shared).

12.Going live

  • Go-live is store by store, not all 50 at once. First 2–3 pilot stores, then waves.
  • Before a store goes live, its stock is physically counted once — that count becomes the opening stock. This is effort, and it is unavoidable.
  • For some time the system runs in parallel with today's Tally/Excel routine — nothing stops until the system has proven itself.
  • Staff training will be simple, in Hindi, mostly short videos/voice notes.
  • Which 2–3 stores are the best pilots (cooperative manager, decent volume, close to Patna)?
  • Any periods where stores must not be disturbed (festival rush, wedding season, audits)? Tell us the calendar to avoid.
  • Full store list — every store/warehouse with name, city, and the person in charge.
