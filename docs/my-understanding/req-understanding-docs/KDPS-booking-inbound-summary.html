KDPS · Client Summary

Booking & Inbound — What We've Designed

The first half of the system: from placing an order with a vendor, to that stock becoming sellable at the store. Plus what we need from you, and which parts of the full system are still ahead.

1.What this covers

One clean spine — order placed, goods received and documented, stock live at the store.

Booking placed → Goods + invoice arrive → Counted & checked → GRN → PT file made → Head office inwards → Split to stores → Sellable at counter
The one rule behind it all
Nothing is trusted until the physical goods, the invoice and the PT file agree. The system makes the data clean and traceable, so accounting and payment can rely on it without anyone re-doing it in Excel.

2.Module 1 — Vendor & Booking

The record of who we buy from, and every order placed against them — live from the moment it's placed, not only when goods arrive.

Three records

  • AAgent — one fixed contact per city, for non-branded vendors. Stays accountable until stock is delivered & verified.
  • VVendor — a brand (direct, no agent) or a non-branded supplier, with its commercial terms.
  • BBooking — one vendor + one season. Lines are style-code → per size: quantity. Never mixes vendors.

Three commercial models

  • 25-18-10 (SOR) — commission: 25% full-price, 18% during EOSS, 10% return allowance per season.
  • Buy & Sell (Outright) — KDPS owns the stock; no returns.
  • Consignment — vendor's goods in our stores; vendor takes back the unsold.

Terms are snapshotted onto each booking, so later changes don't rewrite history.

How a booking is created & received

  • Branded: upload the vendor's receiving slip → the system reads style/size/quantity and drafts the booking → handler confirms.
  • Non-branded: typed in by hand (verbal/agent order), with an option to upload a photo to auto-fill.
  • Receiving → GRN: when goods arrive, the order list pre-fills a screen; the receiver enters what physically came in, notes anything short or damaged, and presses Generate GRN. One booking can have many GRNs over several arrivals.
  • Over-receipt (extra items/sizes) → add a line with a required remark. Damaged → received but quarantined (not sellable), routed to the defective flow.

Returns & the SOR safety net

  • 25-18-10 (SOR): return applied manually within the window; 10% allowance pooled per vendor per season; system alerts at 30 / 15 / 7 days before the deadline.
  • Buy & Sell: no returns — closes at fully-received.
  • Consignment: vendor collects the unsold.

Every edit needs a remark; nothing auto-closes; a full audit trail is kept.

3.Module 2 — Inbound Ingestion

Turning a real-world arrival into clean, accounting-ready stock — branded and non-branded handled differently.

The Shipment is the anchor

One vendor arrival = one Shipment. Its invoice, GRN, PT file, store-split and accounting export all hang off it, each with its own status. (Distinct from the Consignment commercial model.)

Branded mostly automatic

Invoice + goods together · every item has a vendor barcode · PT file retrievable when asked.

  • Store delivery (common): store counts & checks → sends invoice + details to the warehouse person, who makes the GRN.
  • Warehouse delivery: counted & GRN'd at the warehouse.
  • Brand PT → KDPS PT is automatic software (fixed per-brand rules). Vendor barcode is kept.

Non-branded AI-assisted

Arrives at the warehouse with an invoice only.

  • Upload invoice → AI reads it → chat shows what's filled vs missing.
  • Operator fills the gaps in chat — colour (the invoice's colour if it states one, else Premium/Medium/Economy), size, call the vendor — then gets an editable Excel PT.
  • Size missing → "Free Size." Untagged price = base + 1.1% transport + GST + 30–35% margin (overridable).

SKU & barcode

  • SKU = the sellable type: Brand + Style-code + Colour + Size. Used for stock, reorder and dead-stock.
  • The barcode is that identity — one barcode covers many identical pieces (5 identical kurtis share one barcode); stock is a count under the barcode, not one barcode per piece. The full key is barcode + season.
  • No silent duplicates: the same non-branded product (vendor + style + size) rolls up to one SKU / one barcode and adds to its count. (Follows your requirement for a central SKU registry.)
  • Same style next season reuses the identity; each season is a separate stock cohort (barcode + season).

Checks, splitting & accounting

  • Two human gates (warehouse approval, then head-office inward): the system auto-checks and flags only problems; a person approves for accountability.
  • Discrepancy never blocks — goods are physically in and must sell; the gap is logged and chased in parallel (account manager closes it).
  • Store split: manual now (every split recorded), AI-suggested later from sales history.
  • Nightly reconciliation: Invoice ↔ GRN ↔ PT. Accounting export generated after head-office inward — exact, no manual reshaping.

How it's built — principles

  • AI only at the edges (reading invoices, the non-branded assistant). Everything to do with money — pricing, tax, accounting export, reconciliation matching — is exact software, never guessed.
  • The app is the record; WhatsApp is the nudge that links back into it.
  • Audit trail is automatic — every action is logged (who, what, when, before → after).
  • POS is not rebuilt — we feed it stock and read sales back via its API.
