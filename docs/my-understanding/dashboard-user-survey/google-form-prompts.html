Google Form prompts · KDPS daily-work survey

Six ready-to-paste prompts for the Google Forms AI builder · one form per role · English · 27 July 2026

How to use: In Google Forms, start a new form and choose "Help me create a form" (the Gemini option). Paste one prompt below, let it generate, then review the questions and publish. Repeat for each of the six roles. Each prompt is fully self-contained.

Confidentiality: None of the prompts mention any new system, software, app, or dashboard. The survey is framed only as management wanting to understand daily work to improve operations.

1 · Owner

For the owners / directors. Focus: what they track across all stores, approvals, targets, and how they want information delivered.
Create a survey form titled "KDPS Daily Work Survey - Owner / Director".

Form description: "KDPS is studying how each of us works day to day so we can improve our operations and reporting. Please answer honestly about your real routine. There are no right or wrong answers. Takes about 10 minutes."

Create these questions in order:

1. Short answer, required: "Your name"
2. Short answer, required: "Which businesses or areas do you personally look after? (e.g. all stores, specific brands, finance)"
3. Paragraph, required: "When your work day starts, what is the FIRST information you check or ask for? Where do you get it from today (phone call, WhatsApp, Excel, visiting the store)?"
4. Checkboxes, required, title "Which of these do you personally track or review during a normal day?" with options: "Total sale of each store", "Cash position", "Discounts given", "Stock arriving from brands", "Stock transfers between stores", "Staff attendance", "Vendor payments due", "Customer complaints", "Expenses", "Other"
5. Multiple choice, required: "How many times a day do you check sales figures?" with options: "Once a day", "2-3 times a day", "Every few hours", "Constantly through the day", "Only when someone reports to me"
6. Checkboxes, required, title "If you could see only FIVE numbers every morning, which five would you pick?" with options: "Yesterday's total sale (all stores)", "Month-to-date sale vs target", "Today's sale so far", "Cash in hand at each store", "Total discount given yesterday", "Number of bills / customers yesterday", "Average bill value", "Stock value in each store", "Goods received from brands this week", "Payments due to vendors", "Slow-moving / old stock value", "Store-wise comparison (best vs worst store)"
7. Paragraph, required: "Which decisions come to YOU for approval in a normal week? (e.g. extra discount, price change, stock transfer, big expense) How do people reach you for these approvals today?"
8. Multiple choice, required: "How do you prefer to receive daily business updates?" with options: "WhatsApp message", "Phone call from staff", "Printed / written report", "I check Excel myself", "I visit stores and see"
9. Paragraph, required: "What do you review at MONTH END or month start? Which reports do you wait for, who prepares them, and how long do they take to reach you?"
10. Paragraph, required: "Is there any information about the business you WISH you could get instantly, but today it takes hours or days to get? What is it?"
11. Paragraph, optional: "Think of the last time a problem in a store was found late (wrong stock, missing cash, too much discount, anything). What was it, and how was it discovered?"
12. Linear scale 1 to 5, required, title "How much do you trust the sales and stock figures you receive today?" with 1 labelled "I always double-check" and 5 labelled "I trust them fully"
13. Paragraph, optional: "Anything else about your daily or monthly routine you want to share?"

2 · Store person (billing / POS)

For store managers and billing staff. Focus: the billing day, cash, customers, stock questions, what they report upward.
Create a survey form titled "KDPS Daily Work Survey - Store Staff".

Form description: "KDPS is studying how each of us works day to day so we can improve our operations. Please answer about your real routine at the store. There are no right or wrong answers. Takes about 10 minutes."

Create these questions in order:

1. Short answer, required: "Your name"
2. Short answer, required: "Which store do you work at?"
3. Multiple choice, required: "What is your main job at the store?" with options: "Store manager", "Billing / cashier", "Sales floor staff", "I do everything", "Other"
4. Paragraph, required: "Describe your normal day from opening the store to closing it. What do you do first, what takes most of your time, what do you do before leaving?"
5. Checkboxes, required, title "Which of these tasks do you do on a normal day?" with options: "Billing customers", "Handling returns or exchanges", "Counting cash", "Checking stock for a customer's size or colour", "Receiving goods at the store", "Sending or receiving stock transfer", "Noting expenses", "Reporting the day's sale to office or owner", "Handling customer complaints", "Arranging / folding stock", "Other"
6. Multiple choice, required: "How do you report your store's daily sale today?" with options: "WhatsApp message to owner or office", "Phone call", "Written in a register", "Excel sheet", "The POS does it automatically", "I don't report daily"
7. Paragraph, required: "When a customer asks for a size or colour you don't see on the floor, what exactly do you do to find out if it is available? How long does it take?"
8. Checkboxes, required, title "Which information do you need MOST often during the day?" with options: "Today's sale total so far", "Price of an item", "Whether an offer or discount applies", "Stock of a size or colour in my store", "Stock available in other stores or warehouse", "Cash in the drawer", "My store's monthly target and where we are", "Pending goods coming to my store", "Customer's previous purchases"
9. Multiple choice, required: "How do you come to know about a new offer or discount scheme?" with options: "Owner or office tells us on WhatsApp", "Phone call", "Brand person tells us", "Written note in store", "Sometimes we find out late"
10. Paragraph, required: "What do you do at month end or month start that is different from normal days? (counting stock, making reports, targets, anything)"
11. Paragraph, required: "What is the SLOWEST or most irritating part of your daily work? What takes longer than it should?"
12. Paragraph, required: "If every morning you could see one screen with the most useful information for running your store that day, what would you want on it?"
13. Linear scale 1 to 5, required, title "How often does the stock on the shelf match what the records say?" with 1 labelled "Often different" and 5 labelled "Always matches"
14. Paragraph, optional: "Anything else about your daily work you want to share?"
