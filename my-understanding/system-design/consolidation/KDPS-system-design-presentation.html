Stock is blind

Money is guesswork

We replace the three worlds with one source of truth — and put AI on top of it.

Module by module

What each part fixes, what it does, and the AI in it

Seven parts of one system. For each: the problem today, what it now does, and exactly how much AI is inside — honestly.

1

Vendors & Buying

The start of the spine
What it solves

Orders live in people's heads. No one knows what was ordered versus what arrived — or when a brand's return window closes.

What it has

Every order becomes a tracked booking, per brand and season. It updates itself as goods arrive (part-received → fully received), watches each brand's return deadline with alarms (30/15/7 days), and knows each brand's deal type — outright, margin, sale-or-return, consignment.

The AI in it

Reads the brand's order sheet and fills the booking for you — you just check and confirm.

2

Goods In

Arrival → ready to sell
What it solves

Every delivery is re-typed by hand into Excel. Stock has no price and no size/colour. Nothing checks the invoice against what actually arrived.

What it has

One flow: invoice → count (GRN) → priced stock (PT) → sent to stores. Stock is tracked to the exact size and colour, with a barcode, priced at true cost. Money owed to the brand is recorded the right way for each deal type.

The AI in it

Reads the invoice into the system and helps build the price sheet for unbranded goods. The price conversion itself is plain, exact software — not AI.

3

Selling & Stock · Offers inside

The end of the spine
What it solves

Each store's POS is its own island. Exchanges, transfers, returns and stock counts are all manual. Discounts run on memory, with no proof the right one was given.

What it has

One engine for sale, exchange, store-to-store transfer, returns, end-of-season sale and stock-count — always knowing what's truly free to sell. Offers live here: each brand's rules applied automatically at the till, best deal for the customer, with a daily check that the right discount was given.

The AI in it

Suggests markdowns on slow stock (never below cost, with a step-down plan); reads messy brand offer sheets into clean rules.

4

Money

Tracked at every step
What it solves

What's owed to each brand is guesswork. No one checks that each store's cash, card and UPI actually reached the bank.

What it has

Every rupee tracked from the first order to the final payment. Vendor dues built from real documents, never typed. Payments go through proper approvals — different people raise, approve and pay. A daily check that every store's money landed in the bank.

The AI in it

Reads bank statements, catches duplicate payments and a changed vendor account (fraud), gives a one-line "safe to pay?" check, matches cash to bank — all suggestions; a person approves.

5

Accounting · Tally

Tracked at every step
What it solves

Everything is re-typed into Tally by hand.

What it has

Ready-made GST entries flow into Tally automatically — once, correctly — with a daily check that nothing was missed. Tally stays your official book; the system feeds it, never replaces it.

The AI in it

None — on purpose. The books are exact and machine-made, never guessed.

6

The Brain · Reports & AI

Reads everything, on top
What it solves

Month-end is built by hand. There's no daily picture, and no early warning when something goes wrong.

What it has

A 9 PM WhatsApp summary of the whole business; forecasts down to size; a dead-stock hunter; profit scorecards per brand and store; a watchdog for leaks and odd numbers; one-click brand reports; and an assistant you can ask, in Hindi or English.

The AI in it

This is where the AI lives. It reads everything and writes only messages, reports and suggestions — and never touches the books.

7

Master Records & Users

The foundation under everything
What it solves

The same lists — brands, vendors, stores, sizes, seasons — are kept differently everywhere, and anyone can change anything.

What it has

One trusted home for every list, with who-can-change-what rules, a second pair of eyes on the dangerous ones (brand terms, tax, vendor bank details, user roles), and a full history. Every document takes a snapshot — so changing something today never rewrites yesterday's bill.

The AI in it

Only helps draft — reading offer sheets, cleaning up duplicate names. A person always confirms.

Designed around AI

AI everywhere it helps — never where it's dangerous

The one rule. AI reads the mess, watches, forecasts and suggests. People and the books make every real decision. AI never moves a rupee on its own — code computes the numbers, AI only explains them, a human decides.

Why build ours

Why this beats buying a ready-made system

The edge: KDPS runs outright, margin, sale-or-return and consignment brands side by side. Ready-made systems treat sale-or-return as an afterthought — a checkbox. Ours treats who-owns-the-stock as a core fact, so the brand's money is only owed when the item actually sells, returns are handled by the deal, and every report is right by brand.

Built for your reality

40+ brands, four deal types, two states and two GST numbers, low-bandwidth stores — modelled in, not worked around.

Tally stays your book

The system feeds Tally; it never replaces it. The leading Indian systems do exactly this — we do it cleanly and automatically.

Best-practice spine

Every design rule matches how the serious systems (SAP, Oracle, NetSuite) are built: documents write the books; the books are never typed; AI only at the edges.

For the team · technical overview

How it's built

— overview only; skip this part in the client walkthrough —

Deliberately boring and proven. One application, one database; hosting to be decided later.

Python/Django engine + typed React PWA · types front to back PostgreSQL · built for ledgers A hosted top model · best model for each job (AI platform to be decided later) Hosting — to be decided later

The stack & why

Python/Django engine + a typed React PWA — exact money maths in the engine (Decimal + Postgres NUMERIC) with a typed client at the API seam, so money bugs get caught before they ship.

PostgreSQL — relational, transactional, built for append-only ledgers; can scope each store/GST number so data is safe by design.

Hosting and region will be decided later. The AI only ever sees safe, edge data and computed numbers — never raw customer or payment details.

How AI plugs in

One read-only "ask the system" layer (an MCP layer) over the agreed numbers. The assistant and the digest fetch through it, in Hindi or English, only within each person's access — and it can never write the books.

Option for later: the same layer can draft an action (a markdown, a reorder) into a human's approval queue — still nothing goes live without a person.

Messaging — options

WhatsApp for owners and managers — where they already live (digests, alerts, the assistant).

Slack (option) for the internal team's working chatter, if wanted, with WhatsApp kept for anything customer- and claim-related.

Build approach

One verified slice at a time — a full thread through the system, proven on real invoices, before widening. Money code is always supervised.

Migration & rollout (moving the data, store-by-store go-live) is planned as its own step.
