Data Model & ERD

The relational schema for the KDPS operating system — the entities, their keys, and how they relate. This is the shape of the database: identity, money, ledgers, masters, documents. It is the picture a developer holds while writing the first migration.

25 June 2026 · Schema-of-record · Encodes the 16 locked Q&A decisions + the money-critical open items. Source of truth: .context/qa-decisions.md, the reconciliation plan, and the 24-Jun review.
This is the schema, not the posting rules. This document says what tables exist and how they relate — the nouns of the database. It deliberately does not say which document posts which ledger legs under which commercial model — that is the posting-catalog.html, the heart of correctness. Read the two together: this gives you the columns; the catalog gives you the rules that fill the ledger tables.

Everything below traces to a locked decision. Five ideas carry the whole model and must never be compromised:

  • Identity is the cohort. A real product is SKU = Brand + Style(Design) + Colour + Size; what we count and price is the cohort = barcode + season. The barcode is a non-unique scan-alias that resolves to a cohort — it is not the identity (Q6, Q7).
  • Money is integer paise. Every amount is a BIGINT count of paise. Never a float, never a decimal-that-rounds. ₹28,50,000 is stored as 285000000.
  • Ledgers are append-only. Stock, vendor and cash ledgers only ever receive rows; a balance is always the sum of its postings; a correction is a new reversing row, never an edit (FD-6, ADR-0004/0006).
  • History-driving masters are effective-dated (SCD-2). Only the five masters whose change must not rewrite an old bill — GST slab, GSTIN, cost, price, brand terms — carry from/to dates. Everything else is current-value + audit + document snapshot (Q11/Q15).
  • Every ledger leg carries its dimensions. Store, brand, season and state-GSTIN are declared foreign-key columns on every posting from day one — scoping and reporting are built in, not retrofitted (Q12, FD-3).

2 · The five schema laws

Conventions every table obeys. These are code-level invariants, enforced by the database and the central posting engine — not coding-style preferences.

① Money = integer paise money-critical

Every monetary column is BIGINT holding paise. ₹1 = 100; ₹28,50,000 = 285000000. No FLOAT, no NUMERIC with a scale that can silently round in arithmetic. Display formats to Lakh/Crore (₹28,50,000) at the screen edge only. Tax, cost, MRP, P RATE, margin, payable, payment — all paise. This makes Σ debits = Σ credits an exact integer test, which the CI gate checks on every posting.

② Append-only ledgers, balance = Σ postings

The three ledger tables grant INSERT only — UPDATE/DELETE are revoked at the role level and blocked by a trigger. No row ever stores a running balance; a balance is computed by summing postings with the relevant dimension filters. A mistake is undone by posting a reversing entry (same legs, opposite sign, today's date, a reason code), never by editing or deleting the original.

③ Documents write ledgers; ledgers are derived

Only a document (GRN, PT, Sale, Payment, …) writes ledger rows, through one central post_entries(document, legs) call that is balanced-or-fails. Humans and AI only read ledgers. Every ledger row carries a FK back to the source document and its line, so any number traces home.

④ SCD-2 only where history drives money

Five masters carry valid_from / valid_to / is_current and a fresh row per change (Type-2): GST slab, GSTIN, brand cost, price, brand commercial terms. Everywhere else, the master holds the current value, the audit log holds who/why/when, and documents snapshot what they used — that is enough, and cheaper to keep correct.

⑤ Dimensions on every ledger leg

store_id, brand_id, season_id and state_gstin_id are real FK columns on every stock, vendor and cash posting — declared from the kernel. App-layer scoping (django-scopes, fail-closed) filters on them; the schema stays RLS-ready. Cross-store isolation is a CI test (Q12, ADR-0003).

Common columns on every table. Every row carries id (surrogate PK), created_at, created_by, and — where editable — updated_at, updated_by. Masters add is_active (soft-delete; nothing is ever hard-deleted, Rule 10). Documents add the skeleton in §9. Ledgers are insert-only and never updated.

4 · Identity — SKU, barcode, cohort Q6 · Q7 locked

The single most load-bearing decision in the schema. Stock is a count under a cohort, never one row per physical piece, and the barcode is an alias, never the key.

The identity chain. SKU (the real product = Brand + Style + Colour + Size) → Cohort (what we count and price = barcode + season) ← BarcodeAlias (the scanned EAN, non-unique, resolves to a cohort). A scan never identifies a piece — it points at a cohort, and the stock is the integer count under that cohort. There is no "one barcode per piece"; that stale phrasing is corrected everywhere (F8).

Two repeat cases, handled in data — never blocked.
  • Intentional reuse — many physical items share one barcode by design (colour collapsed to a P/M/E tier; same price/brand/style/size). Genuinely interchangeable: a sale just reduces the count. No ambiguity (Q7a).
  • Accidental collision — two different-price SKUs share a vendor barcode. Caught at inbound (flag + assign an internal barcode via the existing generator — 68% have no vendor barcode anyway). Targeted, never a blanket re-barcode (Q7b).
At the POS edge: one match → post; many → oldest-season-first; ambiguous → one-tap "which season?". Never block the sale (Q6).

6 · Commercial model — the two-axis registry Q10 locked

The first-class ownership dimension. We do not store the friendly label (Outright/Correction/SOR/Consignment) as the source of truth — we store the two axes plus the timing attributes, snapshot them per brand per season, and derive the label for screens.

Store the axes, derive the label. Two brands the client describes differently can land on the same record; one client answer can't break the data model. Screens still read "Outright / Correction / SOR / Consignment" — that string is computed from the axes, never typed.

8 · SCD Type-2 effective dating Q11 / Q15 locked

Versioning is applied only at the grain that drives money history. Over-versioning everything is the wrong kind of complexity; under-versioning these five rewrites old bills. These exactly are the SCD-2 masters:

SCD-2 masterWhy it must be effective-datedGrain of the version
GST slabA slab change (e.g. 22 Sep 2025) must not re-tax a bill from before it.By effective date.
GSTINA store's GSTIN change must leave old vouchers on their original number.By effective date.
Cost (brand cost / landed)Cost is frozen at stock-in and read by all profitability; a later cost edit must not move an old margin.By stock-in event (and the cohort holds the frozen value).
Price (MRP / sell)An old bill carries the price it sold at, not today's.By effective date / season.
Brand termsMargin/model/return-window per brand; yesterday's booking keeps yesterday's deal.By season (the season is the version).
The standard SCD-2 columns on each of these tables: valid_from (date), valid_to (date, NULL = open), is_current (bool, exactly one true per natural key). A change inserts a new row and closes the old one's valid_to — it never updates the value in place. A document that needs the value resolves it by (natural key, document date).
Everything else is not versioned — by design. Vendor, store, brand, taxonomy, offers, users, roles hold a current value; their history lives in the audit log (who/why/when) and in the snapshots documents take. This is "version only at the grain that matters" (D8 Rule 4) — simple, not over-built, and finer dating can be layered on later without rework.

10 · The PT line — the product spine

One PT row carries product identity, classification, pricing, cost, margin and tax together — so the masters normalise out of it and the two core postings (priced stock-in, vendor liability) compute from it. The model is built outward from this row. It is the denormalised spine — wide on purpose.

Two-step posting (Q1 / F1) — the PT line is read in two passes. The GRN posts the quantity movement (qty, location, condition, season, owned-flag provisional, no unit cost). The PT at Patna inward posts the valuation (unit cost stamped, owned-flag finalised) plus vendor liability per the commercial model. So the stock ledger gets quantity at the GRN and value at the PT. The exact legs are in the posting catalog, not here.

12 · Money & the SOR clocks money-critical

The schema must carry the integrity guards that cannot be tested away later — the GST single-recognition invariant and the two statutory clocks on a SOR/Consignment lot.

SorLot — the lot under two clocks

ColumnTypeNotes
idPKOne lot per SOR/Consignment receipt.
cohort_id, vendor_id, brand_id, season_idFKWhat & whose.
removal_datedateThe single date both clocks compute off.
commercial_return_duedateClock 1 — the 60–120-day commercial return window (from BrandTerms), alarmed 30/15/7.
deemed_supply_duedateClock 2 — the statutory 6-month deemed-supply clock (CGST §31(7)), computed as removal_date + 6 calendar months, alarmed 30/15/7. A distinct clock, greenfield.
gst_recognised(date, voucher_no) — nullableThe single-recognition invariant (F9). Once GST is recognised at the point set by gst_recognition_point, this is stamped; it can be recognised once only. Prevents the arrival-voucher + settlement-journal double count.
qty_remainingintDrives settlement and the return exposure.
Money-critical open items the schema is shaped to hold (closed with the CA before the money slices, per the reconciliation plan):
  • F9 · GST recognised once — the gst_recognised=(date, voucher_no) guard above; one convention per brand, keyed by gst_recognition_point.
  • Two clocks — both modelled off one removal_date; never collapsed into one field.
  • Late freight after PT — no reposting; a forward-only rate-adjust or a separate cost-correction event, never an edit of the frozen cost.
  • Sold-before-PT — a memo value vs block-the-sale decision; the schema must let a sale reference a cohort whose PT valuation is still pending.
  • No-reposting — every correction is a new today-dated event + reason code, linked via voucher_detail_no. Append-only is a code-level invariant (REVOKE UPDATE/DELETE + trigger), not a convention.
