Outbound — System Design

Everything that happens to a piece of stock after it is sellable: sale, exchange, transfer, defective return, seasonal return, EOSS, stocktake. Picks up where Inbound ends (allocation pushed to POS).

1 · The one idea

Outbound is one machine, not seven features. Every way stock leaves or changes hands is the same shape: a document is raised → it locks or moves stock in our ledger → it posts the money → it leaves a named, audited trail. Sale, transfer, return, EOSS — they differ only in what the document does, never in the pattern.

Three rules the whole machine obeys

  • Documents write the ledger. Nobody types a stock level; a document changes it.
  • Flag, don't block. Goods physically present stay sellable; gaps are flagged and chased, never a roadblock.
  • Every change has a name on it. Actors are auto-stamped from logins (no shared accounts); the audit trail is a byproduct, not a chore.

2 · The core model

2.1 The stock position

Our system is the book of record; POS is the sale counter. Stock is held per barcode + season + location:

Free-to-sell = Physical on-hand − everything locked by an open document.

Physical is the honest count. "Locked" is whatever a transfer-in-flight, a defect ticket or a pending return is holding. The number we push to POS is free-to-sell, so POS can never sell a piece that's already promised.

3 · The POS boundary swappable

Ten Software's APIs aren't ready, so every POS touchpoint goes through one adapter. If their APIs arrive, we wire them; if not, we build a KDPS-owned POS behind the same contract and swap it in — nothing else changes. The join key both sides agree on is the barcode.

What the POS sale report actually gives us real data

From SALE REPORT FORMAT.xlsx: Bill Date · Bill No · Customer · Phone · Item · Brand · Color (=P/M/E) · Size · Design No · Barcode · SalesMan (cashier) · Sub Category · Gender · Fit · season · Qty · Rate (MRP) · Gross · Disc% · Disc Amt · Net · Bill Amount · Cash / Card / Credit.

Cadence: near-real-time poll (POS-agnostic) + an authoritative EOD batch that is the daily close and the negative-SOH catch. Real-time push is the destination once we own the POS. Not in the report: funding source, approver, offer-rule id — derived (funding) or target-only (approver/rule).

Banked for the KDPS-built POS: offline-first (queue + sync; cloud-down resilient) · auto-inward-on-scan (selling a not-yet-inwarded allocated piece inwards + bills in one scan) · exchange as a first-class transaction · local price/offer cache · per-transaction risk caps · multi-terminal sync.

1 · Counter sale

Trigger
Barcode scanned at POS, bill printed.
Flow
We pull the sale-report line (near-real-time poll + EOD batch).
Stock
Physical −1; comes off available.
Money
Revenue booked. SOR/Consignment → vendor payable accrues now. Outright/Correction already booked at inbound.
Accountability
Cashier (SalesMan) on every line; discount captured from the report.

2 · Manual-bill fallback

Trigger
POS down (cloud-first → dies offline) or hangs mid-sale.
Flow
Serve on paper now; re-enter into POS before EOD; one-tap "on paper billing" flag.
Safety net
EOD reconciliation flags a paper bill never re-entered (missing sale) or a POS-hang duplicate → HO exception.
Not a new event
It's resilience — lean on reconciliation, no counter friction.

3 · Customer exchange

Document
One Exchange = a return leg + a sale leg, linked to the original bill, reason-coded.
Stock
Returned piece +1 → good back to available, defective → quarantine (event 5). New piece −1.
Value
Not MRP-to-MRP. Credit = what the customer actually paid; new item at its live offer price. Net = difference (pay it, or add items to cover a gap).
POS
Likely pulled as return + sale, paired by the original-bill reference. First-class in the KDPS POS.

4 · Inter-store transfer MBO only

Document
Two-step transfer order (industry standard), raised in our system, pushed to POS.
Dispatch
Source pieces held (not sellable) + enter in-transit; destination shows pending-receipt. Mandatory transport ref (mode + bus/driver/courier).
Receive
Destination scans to receive → becomes available. Dispatched − received gap flagged on the open doc, owned by a named receiver.
Alert
In-transit aging: not received within N days → flagged (catches transit loss).

5 · Defective / GR return to brand

Document
RTV — Credit or Replacement, reason-coded, raised from quarantine.
Money
Outright/Correction (owned) → tracked brand credit (Correction draws the GR allowance). SOR/Consignment (brand-owned) → just returns, recorded.
Routes
Madura → direct from store (light handover ack); others → consolidate at warehouse (that leg is a transfer, event 4) → one RTV from the warehouse.
Replacement
Brand's replacement comes back as inbound, linked to the RTV.

6 · SOR seasonal return

Document
Seasonal Return — same engine as RTV but good stock, no quarantine; bulk by brand + season.
Window
Driven by the booking's return deadline; alerts at 30 / 15 / 7 days; aging shows what's about to expire.
Money
SOR: uncapped, no money owed (never sold). Correction: draws the 25-18-10 GR allowance; over-cap → the V-flip.

7 · EOSS & the V-flip

Counter EOSS
Discount keyed at POS today → captured from the sale report (Disc%/Amt). Target: offer rulebook (D5) pushes offers → POS auto-applies + enforces approval above a floor limit.
Who decided it
Brand-decided vs KDPS-decided isn't in the POS report → derived from the offer rule (D5). Either way the discount just lowers the price → KDPS earns less margin; nothing is claimed back from the brand. The tag is kept only to attribute that margin per brand in reports. (A normal discount is not a claim — corrected 18 Jun.)
The V-flip
Dead stock past the return window the brand won't take back: report it sold under EOSS → claim the brand settlement → flip ownership to KDPS (keep the real brand, set owner=KDPS + status=liquidation, show "V Mufti" as a display label, barcode unchanged) → drops out of brand reports → sell off as ordinary KDPS stock.

8 · Stocktake & negative-SOH

Counting
Rolling cycle counts, ABC-prioritised, blind (expected revealed only after entry). Periodic full count still possible.
Variance
Within tolerance → auto-adjust + log. Above → recount by a second person → reason-coded Adjustment → named approver.
Negative-SOH
Prevent live via auto-inward-on-scan. EOD recon: explained by a known allocation → auto-create the missing inward; unexplained → HO exception. Never persists.
KPI
95–98% record accuracy; 99%+ on A-items.

5 · Cross-cutting rules

Identity & season

The sellable identity is Brand + Style + Colour + Size = the barcode. The style identity is reused across seasons (for reorder & carryover), but each season is a distinct cohort (keyed by barcode + season). Aging, dead-stock, the SOR window and the V-flip all run per cohort — SS-25 never mixes with SS-26.

Reason codes everywhere

Exchanges, returns, RTVs and adjustments all carry a reason code (didn't fit · damaged · mislabel · shrinkage · unsold · unknown…) — standard practice, and it feeds analytics.

Reconciliation & alerts

The EOD batch reconciles our ledger vs POS SOH (negative-SOH), vs paper bills (manual fallback), and dispatched-vs-received (transfers). Time-based alerts: return windows 30/15/7, in-transit aging, dead-stock past window → V-flip.

7 · How it connects

  • Inbound → Outbound: outbound spends the same barcode+season identity and the same POS edge; the stock-in unit cost (frozen at inbound) marries POS revenue for profitability.
  • Booking → Outbound: SOR/Correction returns draw down the return window + allowance set on the booking.
  • Outbound → D4 (payments): sale-side payables (SOR/Consignment), brand credits (RTV), and EOSS/V-flip settlements all feed the payments module.
  • Outbound → D5 (offers): the offer rulebook authors the EOSS rules this module applies and audits.
Feeds back to the Inbound doc: (1) non-branded colour — use the invoice's colour if stated, else P/M/E; (2) barcode source — POS generates it today, we integrate that generator; (3) season/SKU reuse resolved — reuse the style identity, season is a distinct cohort.

Glossary

Book of record
Our system holds the authoritative stock + financial ledger; POS is a downstream sale terminal we reconcile against.
Free-to-sell
Physical on-hand minus everything locked by an open outbound document; the number pushed to POS.
In-transit
Stock that has left the source store but not yet been received at the destination — held by the open transfer document.
RTV
Return-to-Vendor — the document that sends defective/unsold stock back to the brand and tracks the credit.
V-flip (billed-but-retained)
End-of-season move: settle unsold dead stock with the brand as "sold under EOSS," flip ownership to KDPS (real brand kept, "V <brand>" display label, barcode unchanged), drop from brand reports, then sell as KDPS stock.
Season cohort
One season's instance of a style, keyed by barcode + season — ages and clears on its own clock, never mixed with another season.
