KDPS — Stock Workflows

Booking → store ingestion for every goods type. Switch tabs to compare the branded and non-branded flows.

KDPS is one business — this is the whole goods-inward process on a single map. Branded and non-branded goods diverge right after booking, then reconverge at PT-file verification and share one path to the store floor. Roughly 70% of volume is branded, 30% non-branded. A non-branded consignment is counted as branded only when it carries the vendor's barcode + tag, a proper invoice, and a vendor PT file; missing any one keeps it in the non-branded flow.

Roughly 70% of stock volume. The brand books 6–8 months ahead, then dispatches the goods with an invoice — most shipments go straight to the store, the rest to the warehouse. Either way the invoice reaches the warehouse, which builds the KDPS-format PT file from the brand's 15-day master PT file. The finished PT file is then sent back to the specific store for that invoice.

Roughly half of all non-branded volume. The vendor pre-prints the barcode and MRP tag — KDPS reads both straight off the tag, and the MRP is capped by the vendor. The PT file may arrive from the vendor or be built from the invoice. When such goods arrive with the vendor's barcode + tag, a proper detailed invoice, and a vendor PT file all together, KDPS counts them as branded; if any of the three is missing they stay in this non-branded flow.
