1What we are building

One system that holds the whole business — buying, stock, selling, money, and accounts — in one honest place.

KDPS today. A fashion retailer: 50+ stores across Bihar and Jharkhand, 20,000+ products, 40+ brands.

Right now the business runs on three separate things that don't talk to each other:

  • A separate billing machine (POS) in each store.
  • Tally for accounts.
  • Excel for everything in between.

So nobody has one true picture. Stock, money owed to brands, and profit are all guesswork pieced together by hand.

What we build instead: one system that is the single book of truth. The store till still rings the sale — but our system records the truth, it doesn't depend on each store's machine. Tally stays for tax, fed automatically.

The three ideas that hold everything together

If you remember only three things, remember these.

1 · Every event is a document

A booking, a goods-arrival, a sale, a payment — each is a numbered slip.

No slip means it didn't happen in the system. Simple as that.

2 · Documents update the books; the books are never typed by hand

Stock and money balances are just the sum of the slips.

To fix a mistake you add a correcting slip — you never rub out the old one.

3 · One row carries all product truth — the PT file

The PT row holds what a product is, its price, its cost, and its tax — together.

Everything else just points back to it.

Picture the system as five stacked layers. Information flows up. Each layer has one job.

3The 12 rules — the laws

Every part of the system must obey these. They are the constitution. Breaking one is not allowed unless we change the rule on purpose.

  1. Every event is a document with a life cycle. Every booking, goods-receipt, sale, transfer, return or payment is a numbered slip. No slip = it didn't happen.
  2. Documents write the books; the books are never hand-edited. Balances are added up from slips. To fix something, add a correction slip — never edit old history.
  3. Master data lives in one place; documents copy it. Prices, brand terms, GST rules live in master screens. A slip copies what it needs, so changing a master later never changes old slips.
  4. Every fact has exactly one owner. Stock → the stock ledger. Sale → the POS. Accounts/GST → Tally. Offer logic → the offer rulebook.
  5. Flag, never block. A mismatch raises a clear “please fix” item for the right person — it does not stop staff from billing or receiving goods (unless truly dangerous).
  6. Calculated numbers are not typed by hand. If the system can work it out — profit, stock age, MRP — a human should not type it.
  7. Outside systems need written rules and daily checks. For POS, Tally, bank and WhatsApp: write down exactly what's sent and received, and check daily that it landed.
  8. AI only reads and suggests. AI may read messy input and suggest forecasts, alerts and drafts — but never writes stock, money, or Tally entries.
  9. Every line says exactly what item it is. Stock lines carry brand, style, colour, size, season, barcode. No vague “100 shirts.”
  10. Every action has an actor. Every slip records who made it, who approved it, and when. Approvals happen inside the system.
  11. Deadlines are data, not memory. Return windows, offer dates, payment due dates, season dates are stored — and the system reminds people before a deadline is missed.
  12. Business differences are data, not code. Brand deals, GST slabs, offer rules, roles, seasons, report timings are master-data rows a trained admin can change — without a software release. Code holds only the small posting engine.

4The words we use

Short, plain meanings. Read once; every later section uses these exact words.

Products & stock

WordPlain meaning
SKUThe real product: Brand + Style + Colour + Size. A red kurta size M is a different SKU from size L. We hold stock at this level — never at “style” alone.
BarcodeThe number scanned at the till. It is just a label, not the identity. The same barcode can sit on many identical pieces, and can even repeat in a later season — so a barcode is non-unique.
CohortWhat we actually count and price: barcode + season. Each season is its own cohort, with its own count, price and age.
Count under a barcodeStock is a count of pieces under a cohort — not one row per piece. Receive 40 identical kurtas → +40 to one cohort. Sell one → −1.
P / M / E tierPremium / Medium / Economy. Used in place of “colour” when a brand doesn't track exact colour.
SeasonThe selling period (e.g. Summer 2025). Tags every item; drives age and markdown. Runs Open → EOSS → Closed.

Documents & the books

WordPlain meaning
DocumentA numbered slip for one event. Only a document can change the books.
PostingAn entry a document adds to a book. Always balanced; never edited.
LedgerA book whose balance is just the sum of its postings. Three of them: Stock, Vendor (what we owe each brand), Cash.
SnapshotThe copy a document keeps of the master values it used (price, brand deal, GST). Editing a master later never rewrites old slips.
Reversing entryThe only legal fix for a posted slip: a new, dated correction with a reason — linked to the original. Nothing is ever silently rewritten.
GRNGoods Receipt Note — records what physically arrived (the count only, no price yet).
PTThe priced stock-in file (the spine). It stamps the cost on each piece and records what we owe the brand.
ShipmentThe physical arrival. It is the anchor of inbound — it may or may not have a booking behind it.
V-flipAt end of season, unsold brand-owned stock is settled with the brand, becomes KDPS-owned, and is cleared. The barcode stays the same.

GST, money & geography

WordPlain meaning
GSTINA state tax identity. KDPS is one firm (one PAN), one Tally company, holding two GSTINs — Bihar and Jharkhand — and GST treats those two as separate “persons.” (Confirmed 26 Jun 2026.)
Cross-state transferMoving stock Bihar ↔ Jharkhand is legally a taxable supply (IGST) + an e-way bill. The system records the move and flags it as cross-state; KDPS produces the IGST tax invoice and e-way bill manually, outside the system (26 Jun 2026). A move within one state is just a stock move, no tax.
HSN & slabApparel GST is slab-based and dated: 5% up to ₹2,500/piece, 18% above (from 22 Sep 2025). Stored as data, never hardcoded.
Unit cost (P RATE)The cost we freeze is the P RATE column on the PT, taken as-is (+ freight share, − discount share). We never strip GST out of it — the P RATE is already a without-GST price.
TallyThe official accounts book. We feed it one-way; it stays the legal book of record.

The till & the brain

WordPlain meaning
SaleThe slip a till raises. It is source-agnostic — it doesn't matter if it came from Ten Software, our own POS, or a hand-written bill.
Ten Software / KDPS POSThe current outside till, and the one we build later. Both sit behind one adapter, so swapping is a one-line change.
Idempotency keyA unique id the till stamps on each sale, so re-sending it after a network drop never creates a duplicate.
OfferBrand-specific discount rules (slabs, buy-2-get-1, gifts). Applied at the till; it only lowers the price we collect.
Flag, don't block / Exception queueA mismatch raises a “please fix” item to its owner and waits in a queue. The counter keeps selling.
AI at the edgesAI may read messy input and suggest. It never writes the books or approves anything.

5The four brand deals

This is the heart of the business: who owns the goods, and when do we owe the brand money? Get this right and most of the system follows.

There are four kinds of deal. But we don't store four “types.” We store two simple dials, and the familiar name is worked out from them:

  • Dial 1 — Who owns the goods? KDPS, or the brand.
  • Dial 2 — What returns are allowed? None / a capped amount / unlimited.
  • Plus one yes/no: does the brand keep refilling sold stock?
Deal (the nickname)Who ownsReturnsRefills?When we owe the brand
Outright (BNS) KDPSNone— At the PT (when goods arrive). Full bill incl. GST.
Correction (25-18-10) KDPSCapped allowance— At the PT, like Outright — but a capped return allowance reduces it later.
SOR BrandUnlimited (unsold)No On each sale, one piece at a time. Never at the PT.
Consignment BrandUnlimited (unsold)Yes On each sale, like SOR.

Why the two-dial trick is clever. A brand might call a deal “Correction,” or just “buy-and-sell with a small return clause.” Both come out as the same two dials (KDPS owns + capped return). So a new kind of deal is a new combination of dials — never new software. The screens still show the friendly names; the books quietly key off the dials. Ownership is what decides the GST.

The deal is set per brand, but can change per lot (26 Jun 2026). A brand can run any of the four models. The dials, the margin % and the return window default from the brand, but a KDPS steward can override them on a single booking or stock-received — so one brand's two consignments can carry two different deals.

The five rules the whole schema obeys

  1. Money is whole paise. ₹1 is stored as 100. No decimals, no rounding surprises — so the books always balance to the exact paisa.
  2. Ledgers are add-only. You can insert a row, never edit or delete one. A mistake is undone by a dated reversing entry.
  3. Only documents write ledgers — through one shared “post” step that must balance or the whole thing fails. Every ledger row points back to its document.
  4. History-dated masters. Five masters carry valid-from/valid-to (GST slab, GSTIN, brand cost, price, brand terms). A slip reads the row that was in force on its own date — so an old bill never re-prices under a new rule.
  5. Every ledger row carries its dimensions — store, brand, season, state-GSTIN. The same columns power who-can-see-what, GST routing, and every report.

7What each event does to the books

The heart of correctness. For each event: what it means, and what it changes. Where the four deals differ, it's called out.

The one rule that unlocks inbound: the GRN posts the count only (goods are sellable day one). The PT posts the value and what we owe later. Quantity and money are two separate events.

GRN
goods arrive		 Count what physically came in. +count at (barcode+season). No price yet. Nothing. Nothing.
PT
pricing at Patna		 Stamp the frozen cost on the stock; record what we owe. Stamp unit cost (= P RATE). Mark owned/not-owned. Out Cor: owe full bill incl. GST now.   SOR Con: owe nothing yet. Tally: purchase voucher (owned brands; SOR/Con is CA-pending).
Store split Send stock from warehouse to a store. −count at WH, +count at store. Same cost. Nothing. Nothing (same state).
Sale
at the till		 One piece sells; take the customer's money. −1 at the store. SOR Con: now we owe the brand for that piece.   Out Cor: nothing new (owed at PT). Cash: the tender taken. Tally: daily sales voucher per store (per-bill if customer has a GSTIN).
Exchange Customer swaps an item; settle the difference. old +1 back, new −1 out. Reverse the old piece's effect, apply the new — per each piece's deal. Cash: only the difference.
Transfer — same state Move stock within Bihar (or within Jharkhand). −at source, +at destination, via in-transit. Nothing. No tax. (E-way bill only above ₹1,00,000.)
Transfer — Bihar ↔ Jharkhand Legally a taxable supply to GST. −at sender, +at receiver. Nothing (it's KDPS-to-KDPS). System records the move and flags it cross-state; KDPS makes the IGST tax invoice and e-way bill manually, outside the system (26 Jun 2026).

The one cost number — frozen forever

  • The cost we stamp is the P RATE from the PT, taken directly.
  • Add this piece's share of freight; subtract its share of discount.
  • Never remove GST from it. The P RATE is already a without-GST figure (it's a flat markup, not a tax). Removing GST would make every profit number wrong.
  • This number is frozen at stock-in and never changes. Every profit, every margin, every dead-stock value reads it.

The two clocks — never mix them up

Every brand deal runs two separate clocks. They look the same; they are not.

DealTally clock — when the law says we bought itOur-money clock — when we may actually pay
OutrightAt arrival.At the PT.
CorrectionAt arrival.At the PT (minus returns).
SORCA to confirm (arrival or sale).Only after the piece sells.
ConsignmentCA to confirm.Only after the piece sells.

So an SOR shirt can be a “purchase” in Tally while still being un-payable to the brand until it actually sells. The two clocks live on two different books on purpose.

8The life of each document

The states things move through. Read the arrows left to right.

Booking   Booked → Partly received → Fully received → (Return-window open — only Correction / SOR / Consignment) → Closed   · can be Cancelled any time
Inbound   Shipment arrives → GRN (count) → PT (price + what we owe)   · booking is the normal path (style, size, qty + optional price/MRP) but never required — goods can arrive with no booking, and the booking can be attached or updated later (26 Jun 2026)
A piece of stock   Available → (Reserved / In-transit · or Pending return · or Quarantine) → Gone (sold / returned / moved)   · what we send the till is “free to sell”
Payment   Draft → Pending approval → Approved → Executed → Confirmed (UTR) → Reconciled   · maker ≠ checker ≠ payer, always
POS sale sync   Till makes a UUID offline → Pending (outbox) → In-flight → Synced   · a re-send returns the same Sale — never a duplicate
Season   Open → EOSS (markdown) → Closed
Offer   Scheduled → Live → Lapsed   · a “default” offer fills any gap
SOR deadlines   two clocks off the arrival date: commercial return window (60–120 days) + GST 6-month clock   · both warn at 30 / 15 / 7 days

9What's left out, and what's next

So you know this page is complete for now — and what it deliberately doesn't cover yet.

Left out on purpose (for now)

  • Integration wiring — the exact push/pull contracts with the POS, Tally, bank and WhatsApp. We're focused on the foundation and the core service layer first; the edges come when we build the slices that use them.
  • POS and Tally are deferred (26 Jun 2026) — not in the current build. POS is the last module: Ten Software will sell API access at a cost plus lead time, so we use their API if it's ready by then, else KDPS builds its own — decided at that point. The Tally/accounting sync waits too.

A few money points still need a CA's word

  • For SOR / Consignment: does GST fall due at delivery or at sale? (Per brand.)
  • The 6-month “deemed supply” clock, the cross-state transfer value, late freight after the PT, and a sale that happens before the PT is keyed.

None of these block the build — they're noted so they're never forgotten.

The tech, in one line

One app over one database: Python/Django behind a React (TypeScript) browser app on the store computer, PostgreSQL, hosting to be decided later. Plain and boring on purpose — the kind a small team can keep correct.
