# Baseline: strong retail domain, thin software vocabulary

Anand authored the KDPS current-workflow doc, so his fashion-retail domain knowledge is deep and first-hand (SOR, EOSS, PT files, MBO/EBO, size×color, Patna HO inwarding). His stated gap is the *vocabulary* — both software-architecture terms (ledger, ACID, decoupling, confidence-based routing) and a few formal business-process terms (GRN, 3-way reconciliation, credit note).

**Implication for teaching:** Don't re-teach the retail reality — he lives it. Teach vocabulary by *attaching new words to processes he already knows*. Lead technical terms with one of his own workflow moments ("you already do this — here's its name"). Keep him in the architect's seat: every concept framed as a decision he'll make on KDPS.
