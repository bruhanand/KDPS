Backend rules cheat sheet

What lives inside the Brain's "decide" stage — and how to review it. The rules are where your money and stock are won or lost.

Two kinds of rules

🔎 Validation

  • "Is the request sensible?"
  • About the shape of data
  • Generic to any app
  • e.g. sku_id present, qty > 0

⚖️ Business logic

  • "Is it allowed by KDPS?"
  • About the business meaning
  • Special to KDPS — your design
  • e.g. SOR window, GST, the four models

Test: true for any shop → validation. Only makes sense for KDPS → business logic.

The non-negotiables

RuleWhy
Rules live in the Brain, not only the screenthe screen can be skipped or faked — a screen-only rule is fake
Never trust the screen's inputvalidate everything in the Brain; assume it could be wrong/harmful
Compute, don't trust money & stockGST, profit, on-hand, totals are calculated — never believed from the request

Flag, don't block

SituationDo
sku_id missing (broken request)🛑 Block 400
cashier not allowed to discount🛑 Block 403
selling below cost🚩 Flag allow + warn
return past the Correction budget🚩 Flag allow + warn
stock −1 (sold before inward)🚩 Flag allow — ledger self-heals

Block = broken or forbidden. Flag = unusual but real. A shop must keep selling.

Two more habits

HabitWhat & why
Rates & slabs as dataGST is slab + date based — keep it in dated rows, not price * 0.05 in code. Rate change = edit a row, no re-deploy.
Rules in a service layerWrite a shared rule (GST, the four models) once in a service; endpoints stay thin and just call it. Don't copy-paste a rule across endpoints — they drift.
🧭 Reviewer's lens for any rule:  (1) Enforced in the Brain, not just the screen?  (2) Broken/forbidden input blocked (400/403)?  (3) Unusual-but-real cases flagged, not blocking the store?  (4) Money & stock computed, never trusted from the request?
