Auth & roles cheat sheet

The lock on the Brain's door: who you are, and what you may do. Most real breaches happen here — this is the page to keep open during review.

The two words

🪪 Authentication (authN)

  • "Who are you?" — proving identity
  • Done by logging in
  • Hotel: showing ID at the desk

🔑 Authorization (authZ)

  • "What may you do?" — permissions
  • Decided by your role
  • Hotel: your room key opens your room

Login flow

#Step
1User sends username + password
2Brain checks it against a hashed copy (never plain text)
3Brain returns a token: signed, says user + role + store + expiry
4Screen sends the token with every later request (Brain is stateless)
5Brain checks the token first; none/expired → 401

Trusting the request's rolereq.body.role can lie → read req.user.role from the token
Screen-only lockgreyed-out button but no endpoint check → fake; check in the Brain
Broken access controllogged-in ≠ allowed this record → check the user may see this store/row (change /stores/7→/stores/3 must 403)
🧭 Reviewer's lens:  (1) role from the token, not the request?  (2) checked in the Brain, not just the screen?  (3) allowed this record, not just logged in?  (4) passwords hashed?  (5) tokens expire?
